## TL;DR

This week's signal is narrow but sharp: LeadDev ran four pieces in a row on the gap between AI adoption and AI strategy — engineers are twice as fast, but lead time barely moved, and the fix is measuring friction instead of productivity. That's a direct challenge to how you're tracking your own Claude Code/Bedrock rollout. The Pragmatic Engineer added a supply-chain security thread worth noting given your regulated environment, but didn't go deep this week. Reddit surfaced one on-topic thread — building a security-conscious team in the AI era — but without comments fetched it's a pointer, not a finding. Will Larson, Charity Majors, Level Up, Camille Fournier, and Lara Hogan were all quiet. Light week overall — most of the real signal is in four LeadDev pieces.

## Tier 1 — Core Reads

- **[AI adoption has left strategy behind](https://leaddev.com/ai/ai-adoption-has-left-strategy-behind?utm_source=leaddev&utm_medium=RSS)** — The core claim: almost everyone is using AI day to day, almost no one has a plan for it. Worth checking against your own org — does your Claude Code/Bedrock push have a written strategy, or is it six teams each figuring it out separately? [READ DEEPER]
- **[Your engineers got twice as fast. Your lead time only moved 4%](https://leaddev.com/velocity/your-engineers-got-twice-as-fast-your-lead-time-only-moved-4?utm_source=leaddev&utm_medium=RSS)** — Individual speed gains from AI don't show up at the team level unless review, QA, and release scale with them — the bottleneck just moves downstream. Directly testable against your own metrics: if Claude Code adoption is up, has lead time actually moved, or just time-to-first-draft? [APPLY]
- **[3 agentic AI questions engineering leaders must answer](https://leaddev.com/ai/3-questions-engineering-leaders-should-ask-about-agentic-ai?utm_source=leaddev&utm_medium=RSS)** — A short, concrete checklist for measuring agentic AI's actual impact on your teams and SDLC, not just adoption rate. Usable as-is in a governance review given how much audit trail your SDLC already requires. [SHARE w/ leadership]
- **[Stop measuring developer productivity](https://leaddev.com/reporting/stop-measuring-developer-productivity?utm_source=leaddev&utm_medium=RSS)** — Argues for tracking friction — where work actually stalls — instead of output metrics that AI tooling has made easy to game or misread. Pairs directly with the lead-time piece above; both point at the same fix. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
