## Local Business & Tech

- **[Asado returning to 727 W. Broad St.](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/07/10/tex-mex-redux-asado-to-reopen-in-same-broad-st-space-it-left-6-years-ago/)** — The Tex-Mex spot is reopening in the exact location it left six years ago. Owner Ian Anderson called it funny but is bullish on the Broad St. area. Good indicator of corridor confidence. `[SHARE]`

- **[$92M South Richmond affordable housing completed](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/07/09/crescent-spy-rock-complete-92m-apartments-project-in-south-richmond/)** — Crescent and Spy Rock finished a 344-unit development: 216 family apartments (The Warwick) and 128 senior units (The Cascade), all income-restricted at <60% AMI. Meaningful affordable housing addition to South Richmond. `[WATCH]`

- **[Richmond's "front door problem" — development governance](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/07/10/guest-commentary-richmonds-front-door-problem/)** — BizSense guest commentary argues Richmond's development approval process lacks alignment before projects reach the planning stage — "optimizing for the wrong kitchen." Relevant backdrop for Diamond District and Coliseum decisions. `[WATCH]`

- **[Diamond District defamation case — trial date set](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/07/10/judge-sets-trial-date-orders-settlement-talks-in-squirrels-owners-diamond-district-defamation-case/)** — March 22, 2027 jury trial date set; court ordered settlement conference first. The Squirrels ownership dispute continues to shadow the broader Diamond District redevelopment. `[WATCH]`

- **[Shoosmith Landfill leak, Chesterfield](https://virginiamercury.com/2026/07/10/officials-push-for-transparency-on-testing-around-shoosmith-landfill/)** — 50,000 gallons/day of toxic leachate leaking; closed-door state/federal meeting July 1. Sen. Sturtevant called it a potential "environmental catastrophe." Significant for Chesterfield residents and real estate. `[WATCH]`

- **[Powhatan Worsham Hamlet mixed-use project denied by planning commission](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/07/09/powhatan-planning-commissioners-recommend-denial-of-35-acre-worsham-hamlet-mixed-use-project/)** — Planning commissioners recommended denial of a 35-acre mixed-use project, citing density concerns.
