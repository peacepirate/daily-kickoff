# Richmond & Virginia Weekly — July 25, 2026
*Coverage window: past week*

## TL;DR
Big local business week: Eli Lilly just paid $38M for the old West Creek soccer complex to build a massive pharma facility, and both Richmond and Chesterfield are sending 1% sales tax hikes to November's ballot to fund school builds (with real estate / property tax cuts as offsets). Weekend-wise, JamPacked craft beer + music festival takes over Kanawha Plaza, Style Weekly's calendar has Bob Dylan with Lucinda Williams and Plan 9's 45th Anniversary Bash, and Wilton House Museum opens a rare mourning-jewelry pop-up. On the biotech side, Activation Capital is doubling its lab footprint downtown at 800 E. Leigh instead of building new.
