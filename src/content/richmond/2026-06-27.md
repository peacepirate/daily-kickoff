# Richmond & Virginia Weekly — June 27, 2026
*Coverage window: past week*

## TL;DR
This weekend, Royal Pig pops up at Morty's with layered dishes worth the trip, and East End Fish Co.'s new sustainable seafood market is open in the East End. 5th Wall Theatre's hip-hop satire "Tambo & Bones" is running now — a sharp, fun evening out. On the business side, Hanover County's data center tax drama is worth tracking for the regional tech ecosystem, and a record-breaking $115M apartment deal just closed in West Creek.

## This Weekend

- **[East End Fish Co.](https://richmondmagazine.com/restaurants-in-richmond/east-end-fish-co/)** — New sustainable seafood market in the East End; Style Weekly calls it "locally sourced fruits de mer at a market near you." Great pick for a weekend family food run. [BRING FAMILY]

- **[Royal Pig Pop-Up at Morty's](https://www.styleweekly.com/king-of-the-monsters/)** — Style Weekly raves about "monstrously delicious, layered dishes." Catch it this weekend while it lasts. [ATTEND]

- **["Tambo & Bones" at 5th Wall Theatre](https://www.styleweekly.com/every-day-im-hustlin/)** — 5th Wall Theatre and Blk Virginia Theatre Alliance present a hip-hop satire on racism and capitalism. Running now — good date night or friend outing. [ATTEND]

- **[Colonial Downs First Annual Corgi Races](https://www.wric.com/news/local-news/new-kent-county/colonial-downs-holds-first-annual-corgi-race/)** — Sunday June 28 at Colonial Downs in New Kent County. Absurd fun, kid-magnet, "wagging good time" per WRIC. Worth the 35-minute drive. [BRING FAMILY]

## Local Business & Tech

- **[Hanover County Reinstitutes Data Center Equipment Tax](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/06/26/one-data-center-project-pulled-and-another-delayed-in-hanover-as-supervisors-reinstitute-equipment-tax/)** — The proposed development near Kings Dominion has been pulled, a second near Ashland delayed, and Hanover supervisors brought back the county equipment tax. Signals a meaningful shift in how Hanover is approaching the data center boom — relevant context for Capital One's regional infrastructure neighbors. [WATCH]

- **[No. 303 Opens at 303 W. Broad St](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/06/26/take-a-peek-inside-the-former-common-house-social-club-now-officially-rebranded-as-no-303/)** — The former Common House has rebranded as No. 303, with coworking space, private offices, rooftop bar and pool, event space, and a wine cellar screening room. Worth knowing about for team offsites or client events in the Arts District. [SHARE]

- **[West Creek Apartments Sell for $115M — Goochland Record](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/06/26/weinstein-buys-west-creek-apartment-complex-from-capital-square-for-115m/)** — Weinstein Properties acquired the West Creek complex from Capital Square in the largest single real estate transaction in Goochland County history. Continued investor confidence in the Capital One campus corridor. [WATCH]

- **[Church Hill Auto Shop Converting to Mixed-Use](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/06/25/1-2m-sale-revs-up-plan-for-mixed-use-conversion-of-longtime-church-hill-auto-shop/)** — A 51-year-old auto shop on Marshall Street just sold for $1.2M and is headed for apartments plus commercial space. Church Hill development keeps accelerating. [WATCH]

- **[Pinky's Owner Inks Distribution Deal for Rio Cheesecakes](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/06/25/pinkys-restaurant-owner-inks-distribution-deal-for-startup-cheesecake-business/)** — Steve DeRaffele launched Rio Cheesecakes earlier this year and just landed a distribution deal. Local food startup momentum worth a share. [SHARE]

- **[Richmond Food News: June 25–July 1](https://richmondmagazine.com/restaurants-in-richmond/food-news/june-25-july-1-2026/)** — Richmond Magazine rounds up a cheesy competition, seafood for all, and more. Quick read for weekend dining ideas. [SHARE]

- **[Sound Hall Studios Opens in Henrico](https://richmondmagazine.com/arts-entertainment/music/sound-hall-studios/)** — New recording and instruction studio hub in Henrico County. A good tip if you have musically inclined kids or know musicians in the area. [SHARE]

## VA-Wide Signal

- **[Virginia Budget Back to Lawmakers with Spanberger Amendments](https://www.wric.com/news/politics/capitol-connection/spanberger-budget-amendments-2026-28/)** — VA-wide: Gov. Spanberger sent the budget back with amendments as the July 1 deadline looms. Includes a ~$3/month RGGI ratepayer rebate. Watch for any tech and energy provisions that land. [WATCH]

- **[Federal Housing Bill with Virginia Roots Stalled](https://virginiamercury.com/2026/06/26/lacking-trumps-signature-a-major-congressional-housing-bill-with-va-roots-still-has-a-path-to-law/)** — VA-wide: The most significant congressional housing bill in decades — with aspects first piloted in Virginia — is on hold because Trump is withholding signature pending a voting access bill. Still has a path forward. [WATCH]

- **[RGGI Budget Amendment — ~$3/Month Rebate for Ratepayers](https://virginiamercury.com/2026/06/26/budget-amendment-would-give-ratepayers-estimated-3-monthly-rebate-from-rggi-funds/)** — VA-wide: 45% of Virginia's carbon market revenue would go back to residents, small businesses, and churches under the passed amendment. Awaiting Spanberger's final sign-off. [SHARE]
