## Local Business & Tech

- **[Diamond District Developer Breaks Ground on First Building](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/05/26/diamond-district-developer-to-break-ground-on-first-building-beside-carmax-park/)** — The first apartment building beside CarMax Park is going up, and the project now includes a new hotel developer and a local restaurant group planning a sports bar and grill. Four new structures total are planned. The Diamond District is finally moving from vision to concrete. [WATCH]

- **[Swift Creek Mill Theatre on Hiatus, Future Uncertain](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/05/26/a-cash-crunch-put-swift-creek-mill-theatre-on-hiatus-now-its-leaders-are-pondering-its-next-act/)** — A cash crunch has put the beloved Chesterfield community theater on pause. Worth watching — if they do ask for community support, this is one worth backing. [WATCH]

- **[Field Trip Bikes Opens in Church Hill](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/05/27/cary-st-bike-shop-owner-opens-second-store-in-church-hill/)** — Joel Alford (owner of ReCycles near the Fan) opened a second shop at the former Wheel Simple/Cyclus space on Clay Street. Good sign for Church Hill's retail corridor. [SHARE]

- **[Phlow Founding CEO Eric Edwards Steps Down](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/05/26/phlow-founding-ceo-eric-edwards-steps-down-replacement-named/)** — Leadership transition at the Petersburg pharmaceutical manufacturer about two years into active API production. - **[Goochland TOD Lawsuit: Judge Rejects County's Full 8,500-Page Filing](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/05/27/judge-rejects-countys-full-8500-page-filing-in-goochland-tod-lawsuit/)** — Residents challenging Goochland's tech overlay district got a procedural win — the judge rejected the county's massive filing. The lawsuit alleges supervisors didn't lawfully explain adoption of the overlay. This case could affect the shape of tech-sector land use in the region. [WATCH]

- **[Inaray Opens Lighting Lab Showroom Near Willow Lawn](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/05/26/inaray-opens-lighting-lab-showroom-near-willow-lawn/)** — The lighting, technology and window treatment company just sold off one division and is opening a new Patterson Avenue showroom.
