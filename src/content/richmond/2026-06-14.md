- **['Tuckahoe Tech Park' data center campus proposed on 900-acre tract in Goochland](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/06/11/tuckahoe-tech-park-data-center-campus-planned-on-900-acre-tract-in-goochland/)** — A Colorado developer is first to test Goochland County's new technology overlay district, proposing a large-scale data center campus. Governor Spanberger backs it; Senate is not yet aligned. Relevant to hyperscale build-out near Richmond. `[WATCH]`

- **[Spanberger backs House budget, but data center tax fight divides Democrats](https://virginiabusiness.com/virginia-cannabis-retail-data-center-tax-dispute/)** — Spanberger supports the House's "expansive" data center commission approach over the Senate's tax-and-standards framework. - **[HCA opening Chesterfield ER, secures state approval for similar facility in South Richmond](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/06/11/hca-opening-chesterfield-er-secures-state-approval-for-similar-facility-in-south-richmond/)** — HCA is expanding freestanding emergency room coverage on both sides of the river — Chesterfield opening imminent, South Richmond approved. Good news for the area's healthcare access. `[WATCH]`

- **[Dominion Payroll founders to step down as co-CEOs](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/06/12/dominion-payroll-founders-to-step-down-as-co-ceos/)** — David Gallagher and David Fratkin step down July 1; Dennis Gallagher (current president, David's brother) takes over as sole CEO. - **[HHHunt comes back with 98-home plan for site near Wyndham](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/06/12/hhhunt-comes-back-with-98-home-plan-for-site-near-wyndham-previously-pitched-for-data-centers/)** — After data center and road proposals fell through, HHHunt is back with a 98-home residential plan for land beside Hunting Hawk Golf Club near Wyndham. `[WATCH]`
