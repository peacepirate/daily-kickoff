## TL;DR

Bedrock keeps building out agent testing and evaluation tooling, with a BFSI-specific case study from TCS. Gemini 4 Carbon went GA and Mistral shipped a new open-weight model aimed at agentic coding and security. Anthropic rounded out Claude's workspace tools with Sheets, Whiteboards, and Data.

## What's New

- **[Introducing Kimi K3.1 Pro on Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/introducing-kimi-k3-1-pro-on-amazon-bedrock/)** — Moonshot AI's Kimi K3.1 Pro is now on Bedrock through the OpenAI-compatible Chat Completions, Responses, and Converse APIs, with explicit prompt caching. One more coding-capable model to bench against Claude on the stack you already use. [TRY]
- **[The agent evaluation playbook: Built-in, DeepEval, and AutoEval on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Evaluations](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/the-agent-evaluation-playbook-built-in-deepeval-and-autoeval-on-agentcore/)** — Breaks down which evaluator (built-in, DeepEval, AutoEval) fits which failure mode: task completion, topic drift, data leakage, skipped steps. Directly applicable to scoring the OCR/RAG pipeline's agent behavior, not just output quality. [READ DEEPER]
- **[How TCS built an agentic test orchestrator with Amazon Bedrock AgentCore](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/how-tcs-built-an-agentic-test-orchestrator-with-amazon-bedrock-agentcore/)** — Turns requirements into governed, executable tests for BFSI clients specifically, with per-tenant isolation and an 88% cut in internal test effort. Same regulated-industry constraints your teams work under. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
- **[Gemini 4 Carbon is now available](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-4-carbon-is-now-available/)** — General availability after a defense-only preview. Priced at $2/$10 per million tokens (input/output) at launch, doubling to $4/$20 on December 1 — landing at Opus 5.5 pricing. [Simon Willison's pelican test](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/10/llm-gemini/) has side-by-side output if you want a quick quality read. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Le Chonk: Built for Coders, Trusted by Defenders](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4-oss/)** — Mistral released open weights for Le Chonk, pitched at agentic coding and cybersecurity use cases. Worth a look if you're tracking open-weight alternatives for any workloads that can't leave your environment. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
- **[Context Engineering with Deep Agents: How To Remember Without Keeping Everything In Context](https://www.langchain.com/blog/context-engineering-deep-agents-remember)** — Concrete patterns (offloading, summarization, skills, subagents, memory) for keeping long-running agents from degrading as context fills up. Same problem shape as your document-ingestion agents chewing through long underwriting files. [TRY]
- **[Your apps can now open in Claude, and Claude can work in them](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic#Your-apps-can-now-open-in-Claude,-and-Claude-can-work-in-them)** — Plus [Sheets, Whiteboards, and Data](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic#Introducing-Claude-Whiteboards,-Claude-Sheets,-and-Claude-Data) — Anthropic is filling out Claude's workspace surface beyond code. Relevant to anyone evaluating Claude for non-engineering work. [SHARE w/ product team]
- **[Tracking Anthropic-discovered vulnerabilities](https://www.vulncheck.com/blog/anthropic-glasswing-november-update)** — VulnCheck's running log of vulnerabilities Anthropic's models have surfaced (the "Glasswing" effort). Useful signal on what model-assisted vuln discovery actually finds in practice, for a team in a regulated industry. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
