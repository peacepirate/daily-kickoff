## TL;DR

Google shipped Gemini 4 Carbon, a new frontier model leading independent evals, with initial access limited to cyber defenders. AWS published a concrete blueprint for governed agent platforms on Bedrock AgentCore — directly relevant to your agentic rollout. Docling shipped an OCR fix in your document-ingestion space, and a widely-read Hacker News post digs into how Claude Code handles background-agent transcripts.

## What's New

- **[Gemini 4 Carbon: Our next frontier model](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-4-carbon-our-next-frontier-model/)** — Google's newest frontier model tops independent leaderboards, but access is initially restricted to trusted cyber-defense users rather than a general release. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Building an Agentic AI Platform on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/building-an-agentic-ai-platform-on-amazon-bedrock-agentcore/)** — A reference architecture for a governed, multi-account agent platform on Bedrock: control plane, per-workstream isolation, separate inference and tool gateways, and a CI/CD evaluation gate before any agent ships. Close to the governance problem you'll face scaling agentic coding across teams. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Accelerating software delivery with agentic QA automation using Amazon Nova Act – Part 3](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/accelerating-software-delivery-with-agentic-qa-automation-using-amazon-nova-act-part-3/)** — As agentic QA spreads across an org, the hard part becomes test ownership and access control, not the automation itself. This installment adds team-based permissions and health reporting. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[The Art of Loop Engineering](https://www.langchain.com/blog/the-art-of-loop-engineering)** — LangChain's case for treating the agent loop itself as the design surface: a good model isn't enough, reliability comes from a harness purpose-built for the task, with instrumentation at each level. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Docling v2.144.0](https://github.com/docling-project/docling/releases/tag/v2.144.0)** — Now replaces broken PDF text layers with OCR output instead of passing through garbage text, plus a new `--heading-hierarchy` flag for structure-aware parsing. Direct relevance if Docling is anywhere in your ingestion pipeline. [TRY]

- **[Claude Code regularly sends each background agent's transcript to its model](https://gist.github.com/flamerix-ai/c3905705cc4205e5de3011fa2e74efce)** — A deep dive (363 points on HN) into how Claude Code's background-agent feature shares transcript context with the model. Worth understanding the mechanics before your teams lean harder on background agents. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[GitHub Octoverse 2026](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/28/github-octoverse-2026/)** — GitHub's annual report: merged PRs tripled to 220M/month in under a year, commits more than tripled to 7.38B/month, new repos doubled to 30.3M/month — most of the jump in H2 2026, attributed to agentic engineering. [SHARE w/ leadership]

## Personal / Side-Project Watch

**[Ollama v0.40.13](https://github.com/ollama/ollama/releases/tag/v0.40.13)** — Liquid AI's LFM3 models, including the `lfm3-audio` and `lfm3-omni` text-to-speech variants, now run on Linux and Windows (previously Apple silicon only). Worth a look if local TTS fits anywhere in the Gods of the Future Past content pipeline.
