## TL;DR
Heavy signal day. Mistral OCR 4 lands (directly relevant to your document AI work), AWS ships a managed Bedrock Knowledge Base for agent-native RAG, and two large open-weights models — Kimi K3 (2.8T) and Thinking Machines' Inkling (975B) — drop within hours of each other. Also: a nasty Codex bug is deleting $HOME directories in full-access mode.

## What's New

- **[Build enterprise search for agents with Amazon Bedrock Managed Knowledge Base](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/build-enterprise-search-for-agents-with-amazon-bedrock-managed-knowledge-base/)** — AWS ships a managed RAG primitive covering setup, retrieval tuning, and production readiness. Directly overlaps with what your card-underwriting ingestion stack does today; worth benchmarking as a possible replacement layer. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Introducing Mistral OCR 4](https://mistral.ai/news/ocr-4/)** — Mistral's next-gen document intelligence model. If it holds up on messy underwriting scans, could simplify your OCR pipeline. Run it against your test corpus this week. [TRY]

- **[Introducing Grok on Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/introducing-grok-on-amazon-bedrock/)** — Grok 4.3 available via Bedrock with configurable reasoning effort, tool calling, structured output, and stateful multi-turn. Another Bedrock-native option if teams need model diversity beyond Claude. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Kimi K3: 2.8T open-weights model](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/16/kimi-k3/)** — Moonshot's largest model to date, self-reports beating Claude Opus 4.8 max and GPT-5.5 high on several benchmarks. Weights promised by July 27. Not immediately actionable at your organization (compliance/hosting), but shifts the open-vs-frontier economics conversation. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Inkling: Thinking Machines' first open-weights model](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/16/inkling/)** — Mira Murati's lab drops a 975B MoE (41B active) Apache-2.0 multimodal model trained on 45T tokens of text/image/audio/video. A 276B "Small" variant is coming. Notable because it's American-lab open weights with a permissive license. [SHARE w/ all teams]

- **[Codex bug: full-access mode deleting $HOME](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/16/bad-codex-bug/)** — OpenAI confirms GPT-5.6 in full-access + no-sandbox + no-auto-review mode can override $HOME then delete it. Sharp reminder for anyone letting coding agents run unsandboxed. Audit team defaults. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Introducing computer use in Gemini 3.5 Flash](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-computer-use-gemini-3-5-flash/)** — Google adds browser/OS agent capabilities to a fast, cheap tier. Extends the pattern Anthropic pioneered with Claude computer use. Worth tracking for future automation pilots. [SHARE w/ product team]

- **[LM Studio Bionic: agent for open models](https://lmstudio.ai/blog/introducing-lm-studio-bionic)** — First-party agentic runtime for open models running locally in LM Studio. Relevant for your local-LLM work and side-project experiments. [TRY]
