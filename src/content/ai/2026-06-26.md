# AI Digest — Friday, June 26, 2026
*Coverage window: previous 24 hours*

## TL;DR
Big day: OpenAI previewed the GPT-5.6 series (Sol/Terra/Luna) and Anthropic quietly released a model called Mythos to US-vetted partners. On the document AI front, AWS posted a direct PDF extraction tutorial and Mistral launched OCR 4. Stripe's write-up on production compliance agents is the most actionable read of the day.

## What's New

- **[US allows Anthropic to release Mythos to 'trusted partners'](https://www.reuters.com/technology/us-releases-anthropic-model-mythos-some-us-companies-semafor-reports-2026-06-26/)** — Anthropic has a new model (Mythos) cleared by the US government for release to vetted US companies, currently in limited access. No capability details yet, but if Capital One's Bedrock relationship qualifies, worth checking with your AWS rep. [SHARE w/ leadership]

- **[OpenAI GPT-5.6 Series: Sol, Terra, Luna](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/26/openai/#atom-everything)** — OpenAI previewed three models in limited access: Sol (flagship), Terra (competitive with GPT-5.5 at 2x lower cost), and Luna (fastest/cheapest). General availability expected in coming weeks. Terra's cost profile makes it worth benchmarking for your document ingestion pipelines. [SHARE w/ all teams]

- **[Production-grade AI agents for financial compliance: Lessons from Stripe](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/production-grade-ai-agents-for-financial-compliance-lessons-from-stripe/)** — Stripe's detailed write-up on their ReAct agent framework for compliance at scale. Covers dedicated agent service architecture, human oversight integration, task decomposition patterns, and concrete prompt caching cost savings. This is the kind of production war story that maps directly to underwriting agentic workflows. [READ DEEPER] [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Build interactive PDF text extraction from Amazon S3](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/build-interactive-pdf-text-extraction-from-amazon-s3/)** — AWS tutorial building a real-time PDF extraction server from S3 using an MCP-style protocol, with a side-by-side comparison against Amazon Textract. Directly relevant to your OCR/document ingestion stack. [TRY]

- **[Introducing Mistral OCR 4](https://mistral.ai/news/ocr-4/)** — Mistral's new state-of-the-art document intelligence model. No detailed benchmark data in the announcement, but given your OCR work, worth a quick eval against your current pipeline. [TRY]

- **[Introducing computer use in Gemini 3.5 Flash](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-computer-use-gemini-3-5-flash/?utm_source=deepmind.google&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=gdm&utm_content=)** — Google adds computer use capability to Gemini 3.5 Flash, following Anthropic's lead. Noteworthy as a signal that computer-use agents are becoming table stakes across labs. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[What happened after 2,000 people tried to hack my AI assistant](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/26/hack-my-ai-assistant/#atom-everything)** — Fernando Irarrázaval ran a live prompt injection challenge against an Opus 4.6 agent. After 6,000 attempts and $500 in token spend, nobody leaked the secrets. Concrete data on Opus 4.6's robustness for agents that ingest untrusted external content (like emails or third-party documents). [READ DEEPER]

- **[MinerU — PDF/Office to LLM-ready markdown](https://github.com/opendatalab/MinerU)** — 70k-star open-source tool that converts complex documents to structured markdown/JSON for agentic workflows. Could slot into your RAG ingestion pipeline as a preprocessing step or Textract alternative. [TRY]
