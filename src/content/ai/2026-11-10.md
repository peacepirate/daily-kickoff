## TL;DR

Anthropic shipped Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, now on Bedrock, the same day as its IPO roadshow. Bedrock also picked up single-shot/agentic retrieval controls for Managed Knowledge Base and a seven-layer reference architecture for AgentCore — both directly usable on your RAG and document-ingestion work. Separately, a third-party AI vendor breach (Fireworks AI) hit Notion's customers, worth a flag given your regulated-industry vendor-risk lens.

## What's New

- **[Introducing Claude Fable 5.2 on AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/introducing-claude-fable-5-2-on-aws/)** — Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 are live on Amazon Bedrock and Claude Platform on AWS, the same day as Anthropic's IPO roadshow. Fable 5.2 is currently ranked first on Artificial Analysis. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
- **[Single-shot and agentic retrieval with Amazon Bedrock Managed Knowledge Base](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/single-shot-and-agentic-retrieval-with-amazon-bedrock-managed-knowledge-base/)** — New `AgenticRetrieveStream` API and `maxAgentIteration` parameter let you dial retrieval effort per request, from single-shot RAG to full agentic retrieval. Direct fit for tuning cost/latency on your underwriting RAG pipeline. [TRY]
- **[Pluggable Agentic AI Framework on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/pluggable-agentic-ai-framework-a-seven-layer-composable-enterprise-agentic-ai-reference-architecture-on-amazon-bedrock-agentcore/)** — Seven-layer reference architecture for enterprise agentic AI, each layer with a stable contract so you can swap tech at one layer without redesigning the rest. Useful if you're standardizing the agentic stack across your teams. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
- **[Docling v2.150.0](https://github.com/docling-project/docling/releases/tag/v2.150.0)** — Adds iXBRL input support, fixes a PII-exposure bug in example output, preserves email body furniture, and applies EXIF orientation on document images. Worth a check against your OCR/document-ingestion pipeline. [TRY]
- **[Prompting Claude Fable 5.2](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/10/prompting-claude-fable-52/)** — Anthropic's testing shows Fable 5.2 extends existing code structure (rather than duplicating it) when the prompt frames the codebase as the model's own to maintain. A cheap prompt-engineering lever for Claude Code rollout. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Fireworks AI security breach impacts Notion and their customers](https://www.notion.com/help/security-incident-involving-a-third-party-ai-vendor)** — A third-party AI vendor Notion depends on had a security incident that reached customer data. Concrete case for vendor-risk review of any AI subprocessor in your own stack. [SHARE w/ leadership]
- **[Introducing Our Smallest, Most Efficient Model: Muse Ember](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/10/muse-ember/)** — Meta's small model for repetitive agent work (tool calls, routing, extraction), priced at $0.10/$0.50 per million tokens — the same tier as Claude Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna. Useful context next time you're picking a model for cheap, high-volume subtasks. [SKIP]
- **[Le Chonk: Built for Coders, Trusted by Defenders](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4-oss/)** — Mistral released open weights for a model purpose-built for agentic coding and cybersecurity. Open weights mean you could eval it locally without a vendor dependency. [SKIP]
