## TL;DR

Quiet day for frontier labs outside Mistral, which shipped a new flagship (Large 4) plus an open-weight sibling aimed at agentic coding and cybersecurity. The most practically important story is a security finding: ChatGPT Desktop's agent mode forwards your local SSH agent to remote sessions, letting a remote run push to your private repos with your own credentials. Simon Willison also has a small, directly-usable pattern for taming AI-written PR descriptions.

- **[Introducing Mistral Large 4](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/)** — Mistral's new flagship model. No Bedrock availability yet, but worth tracking if it lands there alongside your existing model options. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Le Chonk: Built for Coders, Trusted by Defenders](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4-oss/)** — Mistral open-sourced weights for a model tuned specifically for agentic coding and cybersecurity tasks. Open weights mean you could eval it locally against your document-ingestion/RAG pipelines without a vendor dependency. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
- **[ChatGPT Desktop forwards your SSH agent to remote machines](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50422628)** — A remote agent session can authenticate with your local SSH credentials and push to your private repos. Direct relevance for any team running agent tooling with broad filesystem/credential access — worth checking what Claude Code's own SSH-forwarding behavior looks like on your setup. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
- **[Hide your slop PR descriptions behind `<details>`](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/9/details-summary/)** — Agent-written PR descriptions are accurate but too verbose for a reviewer to want to read every time. Collapsing them behind a `<details>` tag keeps the full rationale available without forcing it on every reviewer. [TRY]
- **[How Stanford Teaches AI-Native Engineering](https://latent.space/p/cs146s/)** — Stanford's CS146S has students work in unfamiliar repos where a coding agent can finish last year's homework in one prompt; the course had to redesign what it assigns and grades. Relevant lens for how you evaluate team skill growth as agents absorb more of the day-to-day coding. [READ DEEPER]
