## TL;DR

AWS spent the week building a governance layer around Bedrock AgentCore — identity via X.509 PKI, runtime tool discovery via Agent Registry, a 2-billion-document multi-tenant RAG reference from Octave, and a new multi-agent decision engine from Bayer Crop Science — all directly relevant to scaling your doc-ingestion agents past a pilot. Anthropic moved fast on the platform side: Code Review and continuous Security scanning both hit GA, Claude Duet got a dedicated safety/privacy doc alongside a Cowork+chat merge for Enterprise, and Agent Skills was donated to the vendor-neutral Agentic AI Foundation — the same home MCP already moved to, which lowers lock-in risk on anything your teams standardize on. The model race kept moving: Mistral Large 4 launched but still carries no benchmark detail after a full week, Kimi K3.1 Pro landed near Opus 5.5/GPT-6 Astra tier at roughly half the price with open weights due November 4, and Google Cloud became the only hyperscaler hosting all three major closed-source model families after adding OpenAI alongside its new Anthropic-managed Claude option. On security, the eMudhra certificate authority compromise forced a scramble across trust stores — Weaviate patched its entire estate in under 17 hours — and Docling shipped two more hardening releases closing resource-exhaustion holes on untrusted document input, worth a dependency bump if Docling sits anywhere in your ingestion stack.
