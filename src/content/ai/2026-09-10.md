- **[Deploying Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B on Amazon SageMaker HyperPod with vLLM](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/deploying-qwen3-8-2-4t-a95b-on-amazon-sagemaker-hyperpod-with-vllm/)** — Walkthrough of hosting a 2.4T-parameter open-weight model on SageMaker with NVFP4 quantization and native speculative decoding. - **[Agentic Search. This sits squarely in your document-ingestion/RAG space for underwriting — worth a direct look at what it does differently from standard retrieval. [READ DEEPER]

- **[DeepSeek v4.1 Flash](https://twitter.com/deepseek_ai/status/2097930608790167907)** — New DeepSeek release, trending on Hacker News (357 points). No deep technical writeup yet, but another open-weight model worth tracking if you evaluate alternatives to hosted APIs. [SKIP]

- **[Quoting Calif Research](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/10/calif-research/)** — Writeup of WeWorm, a zero-click worm spreading through WeChat calls, where AI found the vulnerability and wrote the first RCE exploit in about two days; the full worm took a week. A small team using AI cut what used to take a large team months down to days. Given your work in a regulated financial-services company, this is a concrete data point for any conversation about AI-accelerated offensive capability. [SHARE w/ leadership]
