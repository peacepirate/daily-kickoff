## What's New

The real news is the model itself: a new frontier-tier release from OpenAI. Worth knowing the name and tier even though your stack runs on Bedrock/Claude — it's the comparison point your teams will ask about. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[The page your agent scrapes is now an attack surface. Is it ready for the hostile web?](https://www.zyte.com/blog/the-page-your-agent-scrapes-is-now-an-attack-surface-is-it-ready-for-the-hostile-web/)** — Argues that content an agent scrapes or browses is now an adversarial surface (prompt injection embedded in pages), not just a data source. Relevant if any of your RAG/ingestion pipelines touch live web content rather than purely internal documents — worth a quick check against your threat model. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[The VMs Powering Mobile Agents (Instinct, Claude Code)](https://rohanadwankar.github.io/posts/platforms.html)** — Technical dig into the sandboxed VM infrastructure behind Claude Code and comparable mobile/cloud coding agents. Useful for understanding what's actually executing under a Bedrock-backed Claude Code session. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Creepy crawlies](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/7/creepy-crawlies/)** — git.kernel.org now spends more CPU rendering commit pages for AI scrapers than on all legitimate access combined, including real git clones. A concrete number behind the "sites are starting to block AI bots" trend — worth keeping in mind for how `fetch_sources.py` identifies itself and paces requests against your own source list. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
