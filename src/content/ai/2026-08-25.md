- **[How we built Claude Code auto mode: a safer way to skip permissions](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/claude-code-auto-mode)** — Anthropic's own account of the safety model behind auto mode: what it auto-approves, what it still gates, and why that's not the same as turning permission checks off. - **[How we contain Claude across products](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/how-we-contain-claude)** — As agents get more capable, the blast radius of a mistake grows too. [SHARE w/ leadership]
- **[Scaling Managed Agents: Decoupling the brain from the hands](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents)** — Architecture pattern for separating an agent's reasoning from its execution environment. Relevant once agentic coding moves past individual devs into shared infrastructure. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Harness design for long-running application development](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/harness-design-long-running-apps)** — How Anthropic designs harnesses for agents doing extended, real development work rather than one-shot tasks. Maps closely to what you're rolling out. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Docling v2.122.0](https://github.com/docling-project/docling/releases/tag/v2.122.0)** — New `compact_tables` option, better title/heading recovery, and a fix that stops line-final hyphens from splitting words mid-PDF-conversion. Direct fit for your document ingestion pipeline. [TRY]
- **[Toyota Scales Enterprise AI with Deep Agents and LangSmith](https://www.langchain.com/blog/how-toyota-north-america-put-enterprise-ai-on-the-balance-sheet-with-deep-agents-and-langsmith)** — 50+ production agents, delivery time cut from 6 months to 4 days, with ROI tracked in LangSmith. A concrete case study for justifying agentic investment to leadership. [SHARE w/ leadership]
- **[LLMs could control their host machines by exploiting inference engines](https://boydkane.com/essays/llms-could-control-their-host-machines-by-exploiting-inference-engines)** — Research write-up on inference engines as an attack surface, separate from the model itself. Worth a look given any self-hosted or local-inference plans in a regulated environment. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
