## TL;DR

Anthropic shipped MCP skill and task support for Claude, plus a separate "Claude Ship" release note with no detail given — worth a look if your teams build internal MCP tooling. LangChain earned ISO/IEC 42001 certification for its AI management system via LangSmith, a concrete governance precedent to cite if your compliance team ever evaluates AI-tooling certifications. Everything else — Gemini 4 Neon's benchmark lead, Zep's multi-hop retrieval API, Google Cloud's agent-trust framework, and a sandboxing security finding — is secondary reading.

## What's New

- **[MCP servers can now ship skills, run tasks, and more with Claude](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic#MCP-servers-can-now-ship-skills,-run-tasks,-and-more-with-Claude)** — Anthropic's changelog lists MCP servers gaining the ability to ship skills and run tasks directly with Claude. No further detail fetched, but it's a direct extension of the MCP tooling your teams already use. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[How We Achieved ISO 42001 with LangSmith](https://www.langchain.com/blog/ai-governance-iso-iec-42001-langsmith)** — LangChain certified its AI management system to ISO/IEC 42001 using LangSmith for the evidence trail. A real-world example of what an AI-governance certification looks like for a company in your position. [SHARE w/ leadership]

- **[Built for trust and control: Powering the next wave of AI agents across Google Workspace and Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/identity-security/deploying-agents-safely-in-your-enterprise/)** — Google's framing for agent deployment in regulated enterprise contexts: access control and data residency as the governing principle. Mostly a positioning piece, but the principle maps directly to your underwriting-data constraints. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Smart Query: multi-hop retrieval](https://www.getzep.com/blog/smart-query-api/)** — Zep's new API plans and runs a chain of searches in one call, for questions that need more than single-shot retrieval. Directly relevant to the RAG side of your document-ingestion work. [TRY]

- **[[AINews] Gemini 4 Neon marks GDM's full return as a Frontier Lab](https://latent.space/p/ainews-gemini-4-neon-marks-gdm-s-full-return-as-a-frontier-lab/)** — Google's Neon model reportedly beats Astra and Fable on LiveBench and cuts a TS/Rust benchmark's runtime by nearly half. New frontier-model data point, no action needed yet.

- **[Three LLM inflection points in twelve months](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/24/llm-inflection-points/)** — Simon Willison's keynote write-up marks one year since Claude Opus 4.5 and traces the major jumps in coding-agent capability since. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Models Can Communicate via Inference Servers Despite Being Sandboxed](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/7wzWuLP3HhyBLmKfo/models-can-communicate-via-inference-servers-despite-being)** — A security finding (245 points on HN) showing sandboxed model instances can still exchange information through shared inference infrastructure. Worth knowing before leaning on sandboxing alone for agent isolation. [READ DEEPER]
