## What's New

- **[Automated Reasoning policy refinement in Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/automated-reasoning-policy-refinement-in-amazon-bedrock/)** — Bedrock's Automated Reasoning checks can now self-diagnose failing policy tests and propose formal-logic fixes for rule or language issues, with every change requiring your approval before it takes effect. Close match for validating underwriting rule logic on Bedrock. [TRY]

- **[From weeks to minutes: How Formula 1 uses agentic AI on AWS to accelerate data operations](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/from-weeks-to-minutes-how-formula-1-uses-agentic-ai-on-aws-to-accelerate-data-operations/)** — F1's Data Accelerator on Bedrock AgentCore cut new data-source onboarding from up to 8 weeks to about 40 minutes, with automated schema evolution and end-to-end observability. A concrete production pattern for automating the tedious parts of pipeline onboarding. [SHARE w/ leadership]

- **[firecrawl/pdf-inspector](https://github.com/firecrawl/pdf-inspector)** — Rust library that inspects and classifies PDFs, detecting scanned vs. text-based pages to drive smart OCR routing. Directly usable in your ingestion pipeline to decide which pages actually need OCR, potentially cutting OCR cost and latency. [TRY]

- **[Quoting Steve Yegge](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/4/steve-yegge/#atom-everything)** — Yegge reports Opus 4.7 introduced a "just two more things" tic that kept an agent from ever converging on real work, and says it's what finally broke his Gas Town project after Opus 4.6 had worked "brilliantly." A concrete practitioner reliability complaint about a model version likely underpinning your teams' Claude Code usage today. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Quoting David Crawshaw's prompt](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/3/david-crawshaw/#atom-everything)** — A one-line nightly cron prompt: fetch upstream changes, rebase local changes on top, verify it still works, and replace the current version. A clean, reproducible pattern for unattended maintenance agents — close in spirit to how your own nightly kickoff pipeline runs. [TRY]

- **[Import AI 467: Self-sustaining AI viruses; pacing AI progress; confusion about AI and creativity](https://jack-clark.net/2026/08/03/import-ai-467-self-sustaining-ai-viruses-pacing-ai-progress-confusion-about-ai-and-creativity/)** — Researchers built a prototype self-replicating computer virus using an open-weight LLM plus a well-designed agent harness. An early but real signal of harness-level security risk as agent frameworks get more capable. [READ DEEPER]
