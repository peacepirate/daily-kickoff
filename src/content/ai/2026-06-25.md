# AI Digest — Wednesday, June 25, 2026

## TL;DR

Mistral OCR 4 drops as a direct competitor to your Azure AI Document Intelligence stack. Google launches Gemini Omni and computer use in Gemini 3.5 Flash. AWS publishes multiple agentic patterns including an MCP-overlay approach for legacy REST services that's directly applicable to your teams.

## What's New

- **[Introducing Mistral OCR 4](https://mistral.ai/news/ocr-4/)** — Mistral claims state-of-the-art on document intelligence. No detailed benchmark breakdown in the summary, but this is a purpose-built document model from a credible lab. Worth a direct comparison run against your current Azure AI Document Intelligence + OCR pipeline on your own document samples. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Introducing Gemini Omni](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-omni/)** — Google's new multimodal flagship. No deep technical summary in the fetch, but a major model release from DeepMind worth tracking as a potential Bedrock addition down the road. [TRY]

- **[Introducing computer use in Gemini 3.5 Flash](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-computer-use-gemini-3-5-flash/)** — Computer use capability (browser/desktop control) now available in Gemini 3.5 Flash. Adds a second viable option alongside Claude's computer use for any browser-automation tasks your teams are exploring. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Retrofit, don't rebuild: Agentic overlays for legacy enterprise services](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/retrofit-dont-rebuild-agentic-overlays-for-transforming-legacy-enterprise-services/)** — AWS details a pattern for wrapping existing REST services as MCP tools and A2A-capable agents without rewriting business logic. This is exactly the low-friction path for making your organization's existing services agent-accessible — no parallel infrastructure, no code duplication. [READ DEEPER] [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Building agentic AI applications with a modern data mesh strategy on AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/building-agentic-ai-applications-with-a-modern-data-mesh-strategy-on-aws/)** — Serverless data mesh architecture for production agentic AI on AWS, covering governance and security. Relevant if your teams are moving from experiments to prod agentic deployments on Bedrock. [TRY]

- **[Build self-service AWS Health analytics with AI agents powered by Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/build-self-service-aws-health-analytics-to-find-actionable-health-insights-with-ai-agents-powered-by-amazon-bedrock/)** — Open-source solution (Chaplin) using Bedrock agents + MCP for self-service infrastructure health analytics. Shows a complete MCP-on-Bedrock pattern you can reference for other internal tooling. [TRY]

- **[OpenAI Codex: median internal token growth 56x in Research, 32x in Customer Support](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-openai-reports-median-internal)** — OpenAI's own internal data on agentic coding adoption since November 2025. Research usage grew 56x, Customer Support 32x, Engineering 27x, Legal 13x. Hard numbers you can use when making the case for accelerating agentic adoption to leadership. [SHARE w/ leadership]

- **[AI and Liability](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/25/ai-and-liability/#atom-everything)** — Schneier on the German ruling holding Google liable for AI overview errors: AI agents are agents of the deploying organization, full stop. If you're building or managing AI-assisted workflows at your organization, this legal framing will matter — the "the AI made the mistake" defense is closing. Worth flagging to product and legal stakeholders. [SHARE w/ product team]
