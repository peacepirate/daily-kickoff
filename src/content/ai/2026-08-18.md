## TL;DR

Docling shipped OCR and page-rendering fixes squarely in your document-ingestion lane. Together AI's DeepSWE benchmark gives a concrete cost/quality tradeoff for coding-agent model routing, and GPT-5.6 Sol got a 50% price cut. AgentCore Payments keeps picking up ecosystem support — AWS shipped an OpenClaw guide, LangChain shipped middleware — worth knowing if agent spend guardrails ever land on your roadmap.

## What's New

- **[Docling v2.120.3](https://github.com/docling-project/docling/releases/tag/v2.120.3)** — Fixes OCRmac coordinate padding, makes page rendering respect DPI, and resolves ODF image references correctly. Small increments, but directly in your OCR/underwriting-ingestion pipeline. [TRY]
- **[DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 vs GPT-5.6 Sol on DeepSWE: Cost, Coding, and Routing](https://www.together.ai/blog/deepseek-v4-pro-0813-vs-gpt-5-6-sol-on-deepswe-cost-coding-and-routing)** — 904 rollouts on a real coding-agent benchmark: Sol leads pass@1 by 10 points at 35x the cost, Pro wins pass@4, and a Pro-first cascade hits 83%. A concrete data point if you're ever asked to justify model choice for agentic coding on cost. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
- **[GPT-5.6 Sol pricing cut by 50%](https://openrouter.ai/openai/gpt-5.6-sol)** — meaningful if Sol shows up anywhere in your routing mix, directly or through a gateway. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
- **[Build OpenClaw agents that transact with Amazon Bedrock AgentCore payments](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/build-openclaw-agents-that-transact-with-amazon-bedrock-agentcore-payments/)** and **[AgentCore Payments middleware for LangChain agents](https://www.langchain.com/blog/langchain-agentcore-payments)** — two more frameworks wiring into Bedrock AgentCore's bounded, human-approved agent payments (x402 protocol). This pattern keeps showing up — worth knowing before an agent needs to pay for anything in your stack. [READ DEEPER]
- **[akitaonrails / ai-memory](https://github.com/akitaonrails/ai-memory)** — open-source long-term memory for agent coding CLIs, built specifically to hand off context between different agent vendors (Claude Code, Codex, others). Relevant if your teams already bounce between tools mid-task. [TRY]
- **[Qwen 3.8 27B scores 52 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/17/qwen-38-27b-scores-52/)** — matches GPT-5.6 Luna and trails GLM-5.2 by one point, despite being a fraction of their size. Already covered in depth yesterday — this is just the benchmark confirmation. [SKIP]
