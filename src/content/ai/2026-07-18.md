# AI Weekly Synthesis — Week of Jul 13–18, 2026
*Coverage window: Mon–Sat*

## TL;DR
A loud week for open weights: Moonshot dropped Kimi K3 (2.8T, weights due July 27) and Thinking Machines shipped Inkling under Apache 2.0. Anthropic reversed course and folded Claude Fable 5 back into Max/Team Premium plans starting July 20 — read that as competitive pressure from GPT-5.6 Sol and Kimi. Bedrock had a strong week for you specifically: Grok 4.3 landed, Managed Knowledge Base for agents launched, and there's a public playbook for a real-estate finance doc-AI pipeline that maps closely onto your underwriting stack. Mistral OCR 4 is worth a bake-off against your current Azure setup. And Codex usage is up ~10x in six months — if you lead agentic coding adoption, that trend line matters.

## Tier 1 — Primary Sources

- **[Introducing Mistral OCR 4](https://mistral.ai/news/ocr-4/)** — Mistral's new state-of-the-art document intelligence model. Direct competitor to Azure OCR in your underwriting pipeline; worth a benchmark on real card statements. [TRY]
- **[Introducing Grok on Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/introducing-grok-on-amazon-bedrock/)** — Grok 4.3 available in Bedrock with configurable reasoning effort, tool calling, structured output, image input, stateful multi-turn. Another model you can hit through your existing Bedrock integration without a new procurement path. [TRY]
- **[Build enterprise search for agents with Amazon Bedrock Managed Knowledge Base](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/build-enterprise-search-for-agents-with-amazon-bedrock-managed-knowledge-base/)** — Managed RAG primitive on Bedrock: simplified setup, smarter retrieval, production-ready. Reduces the amount of hand-rolled Azure AI Search plumbing you'd need to maintain. [TRY]
- **[Built Technologies builds an AI-powered document intelligence solution on AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/built-technologies-builds-an-ai-powered-document-intelligence-solution-on-aws-to-power-agents-across-real-estate-finance/)** — Classify, split, extract, evaluate, reason over 100s of doc types; days-to-minutes turnaround. Closest public reference architecture to what you're building for small-business card underwriting. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
- **[Kimi K3 2.8T-A50B: the largest open model ever released; Opus 4.8-class at Sonnet 5 pricing](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-kimi-k3-28t-a50b-the-largest)** — Moonshot's largest model to date. Currently API-only; open weights promised by July 27. Self-reported benchmarks put it above Opus 4.8 max and GPT-5.5 high, below Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol. Meaningful for cost/perf conversations. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Inkling: Thinking Machines' first open-weights model](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/16/inkling/#atom-everything)** — 975B total / 41B active MoE, Apache 2.0, multimodal (text/image/audio/video), 45T training tokens. First real US open-weights contender at frontier scale in a while.
- **[Claude Fable 5 returning to Max/Team Premium plans July 20](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/18/claude-make-fable-5-permanent/#atom-everything)** — Anthropic reversed the plan to make Fable 5 API-only. Included at 50% of limits on Max/Team Premium; Pro/Team Standard get $100 credit + usage-credit access. Subscription economics matter for team-scale rollouts. [SHARE w/ leadership]
- **[How Smartsheet built a remote MCP server on AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/how-smartsheet-built-a-remote-mcp-server-on-aws/)** — Architecture, security, governance, scaling, deployment for a production MCP server on AWS. Reference material if you deploy MCP servers inside your organization's infra. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
- **[Agentic vision: Building visual intelligence with Amazon Bedrock and MCP servers](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/agentic-vision-building-visual-intelligence-with-amazon-bedrock-and-mcp-servers/)** — Computer Vision MCP Server pattern. Adjacent to your OCR/doc-AI work if you need image-based reasoning steps.
- **[Introducing computer use in Gemini 3.5 Flash](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-computer-use-gemini-3-5-flash/)** — Google's Flash tier gets computer-use. Puts price/latency pressure on Anthropic's computer use and OpenAI Operator.
- **[Introducing Gemini Omni + Nano Banana 2 Lite + Gemini Omni Flash](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-omni/)** — Google's new omni-modal family. Nano Banana 2 Lite and Omni Flash are the "start building today" tiers.

## Tier 2 — Curator Picks

- **[5 Trends That Defined AI Engineering at World's Fair 2026](https://www.latent.space/p/aiewf26trends)** — Framing shift: teams are building systems *around* agents, not just *with* them. Useful talking-points source for pitching agentic adoption up the chain. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Codex usage up >10x in 6 months to 7M users, +1M in past ~day; did Codex overtake Claude Code?](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-codex-usage-up-10x-in-6-months)** — Latent Space's numeric read on the coding-agent race.
