## TL;DR

Heavy AWS/Bedrock day — three posts map almost exactly onto the governance and RAG problems you already own in a regulated shop. Google and Mistral both shipped new models: a calibrated decision-scoring model and an open agentic-coding model. One GitHub repo is worth a direct look — a production skills library for coding agents with 116k stars.

## What's New

- **[Your apps can now open in Claude, and Claude can work in them](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic#Your-apps-can-now-open-in-Claude,-and-Claude-can-work-in-them)** — Claude can now open third-party apps and act inside them, not just chat about them. Early step toward agents embedded in the tools your teams already use, not a separate chat window. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Gemini Decisions API](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/)** — Google's new "Gemini Decision 1" model, a cheaper, calibrated model built just for yes/no, multiple-choice, and rubric-scoring decisions — joining OpenAI and Jev in a new "decision model" category. If any step in your underwriting pipeline is really a classification decision dressed up as a full LLM call, this category is worth watching. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Introducing Mistral Large 4](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/)** — Mistral's new flagship model, alongside open weights for "Le Chonk," pitched at agentic coding and cybersecurity use. Another serious open-weight option if you ever need something outside Bedrock. [READ DEEPER]

- **[addyosmani/agent-skills](https://github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills)** — a curated, production-grade skills library for AI coding agents, 116k+ stars. Directly usable as a seed for the internal skill libraries you're building out across teams. [TRY]

- **[OpenAI Codex on Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/openai-codex-on-amazon-bedrock/)** — AWS's guide for enterprises rolling out Codex with Bedrock as the model backend: access, governance, and cost decisions for platform/security/finance. Same checklist you'd walk through for any second coding agent on Bedrock. [SHARE w/ leadership]

- **[How DXC Technology built a multi-model AI substrate on Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/how-dxc-technology-built-a-multi-model-ai-substrate-on-amazon-bedrock/)** — DXC (regulated IT services, 70 countries) separated a resilient Bedrock model layer from a harness that orchestrates, governs, and audits agents. Close analog to the governance model a regulated-industry EM needs across teams. [SHARE w/ leadership]

- **[Accelerating generative AI delivery with reusable assets](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/accelerating-generative-ai-delivery-with-reusable-assets/)** — AWS's Generative AI Innovation Center published 23 open-source, production-ready components: RAG pipelines, document processors, agent scaffolding, with results from DoorDash, Lyft, and Ricoh. Reusable building blocks instead of hand-rolling your OCR/RAG pipeline pieces. [TRY]

- **[Introducing new AI functions for AlloyDB: ai.classify and ai.extract](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/databases/ai-classify-and-ai-extract-functions-in-alloydb-ai/)** — in-database functions to classify and extract structured data from unstructured text (notes, invoices) without the export-prompt-retry loop. Worth comparing to your current document AI pipeline's operational overhead, even off Google Cloud. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Introducing Claude Whiteboards, Claude Sheets, and Claude Data](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic#Introducing-Claude-Whiteboards,-Claude-Sheets,-and-Claude-Data)** — new Claude.ai surfaces for spreadsheets, whiteboards, and data work. Consumer-facing; no action needed. [SKIP]
