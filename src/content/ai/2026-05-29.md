# AI Digest — Friday, May 29, 2026
*Coverage window: previous 24 hours*

## TL;DR
Google dropped two models at I/O 2026 — Gemini 3.5 and Gemini Omni — while DeepSeek released a V4 preview claiming top-tier reasoning and a major agent capability jump. Mistral also launched Medium 3.5 with remote coding agents. The MCP ecosystem debate surfaced on HN with a skeptical "MCP is dead?" post — worth reading before you deepen team adoption.

## What's New

- **[Gemini 3.5: frontier intelligence with action](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-5/)** — Google's I/O 2026 model drop includes Gemini 3.5 (reasoning + action) and [Gemini Omni](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-omni/) (multimodal flagship). Google is explicitly framing these as agentic-capable — their competitive answer to Claude and GPT-4o. Useful to know as you make the Claude Code case to leadership. [READ DEEPER]

- **[DeepSeek-V4 Preview](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/8bxXqS2R8Fx5-1TLDBiEDg)** — DeepSeek ships a V4 preview claiming world-class reasoning and significantly improved agent capabilities. DeepSeek models tend to land on Bedrock and open-source within weeks; worth tracking as a future cost-curve benchmark for your RAG and inference workloads. (Link is WeChat/Chinese.) [READ DEEPER]

- **[Comprehensive observability for Amazon SageMaker AI LLM inference](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/comprehensive-observability-for-amazon-sagemaker-ai-llm-inference-from-gpu-utilization-to-llm-quality/)** — AWS post on unified Grafana dashboards covering GPU utilization through LLM output quality for SageMaker endpoints with inference components. The observability pattern (quantitative + qualitative in one view) applies directly to your Bedrock setup, even if you're not on SageMaker. [TRY]

- **[MCP is dead?](https://www.quandri.io/engineering-blog/mcp-is-dead)** — 106-point HN post and 85-comment debate questioning MCP's real-world adoption trajectory. Before you evangelize MCP across your 6–10 teams, this is the skeptic case you'll need to know how to answer. The comments are signal-dense. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Remote agents in Vibe — powered by Mistral Medium 3.5](https://mistral.ai/news/vibe-remote-agents-mistral-medium-3-5)** — Mistral ships a new model (Medium 3.5) with remote coding agents in the Vibe IDE, plus a "Work mode" for complex multi-step tasks. More competition in the agentic coding space; if any of your teams are evaluating alternatives to Claude Code, this is now a legitimate option. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[run-llama/liteparse](https://github.com/run-llama/liteparse)** — LlamaIndex's open-source document parser (7.4k stars, trending). Directly relevant to your OCR/document ingestion pipeline — worth a quick eval run against your current Azure AI Document Intelligence pre-processing to see if it handles your doc types faster or more accurately. [TRY]

- **[EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin)** — Official plugin for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and more (18k stars). Adds compound engineering patterns to agentic coding tools. Since you're driving Claude Code adoption, knowing what the plugin ecosystem looks like is part of the story. [TRY]
