[SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Introducing cross-Region inference for OpenAI GPT-5.6 models on Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/introducing-cross-region-inference-for-openai-gpt-5-6-models-on-amazon-bedrock/)** — GPT-5.6 (Sol, Terra, Luna) now available in 25+ AWS regions via Bedrock, with cross-Region inference profiles for throughput. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[AWS vector solutions: Build agentic AI where your data lives](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/aws-vector-solutions-build-agentic-ai-where-your-data-lives/)** — decision framework across six AWS-native vector search options, no standalone vector DB or migration required. Relevant to the underwriting RAG pipeline. [READ DEEPER]
