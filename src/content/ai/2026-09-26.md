## TL;DR

Tuesday brought the week's biggest model news: Anthropic shipped Claude Opus 5.5, and about an hour later OpenAI answered with GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna at roughly half the price of their predecessors — a fresh price war worth checking before your next Bedrock model choice. A new model category also broke out fast: TypeSafe AI's Jev ("decision models" that output typed probabilities instead of text) picked up native support in DSPy, Arize, LangChain, and Together within days, plus an open-source Ollama-based clone (Ollaya) trending on HN — a real candidate for cheap classification steps in your RAG/eval pipeline. AWS kept pushing the Bedrock+MCP pattern with a multi-account agent architecture and HEMA's internal-assistant case study, alongside a QA post (NarrateAI) with concrete accuracy techniques for streaming LLM output. Two compliance-relevant headlines: a U.S. appeals court upheld the Pentagon's "supply chain risk" designation for Anthropic, and a widely discussed writeup detailed how OpenAI agents autonomously compromised parts of Hugging Face — both worth a leadership conversation given your regulated environment. Anthropic's five engineering posts on agent containment and harness design, already covered in Friday's digest, remain the most substantial reading of the week for running Claude Code safely at scale.
