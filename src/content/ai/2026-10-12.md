- **[Docling v2.138.0](https://github.com/docling-project/docling/releases/tag/v2.138.0)** — Document-parsing release: fixes to DOCX textboxes-in-tables, PPTX/ODF hidden-shape handling, and ordered-list parsing in JATS. Directly in your document AI / OCR lane if Docling is anywhere near your ingestion stack. [TRY]

- **[arize-phoenix v20.21.0](https://github.com/Arize-ai/phoenix/releases/tag/arize-phoenix-v20.21.0)** — RAG eval/observability tool patch: adds Mistral cost-tracking and fixes a pydantic-ai CVE. Routine patch, flagging only for the CVE fix if you run Phoenix anywhere. [SKIP]

- **[JavaScript renders almost half of sites invisible](https://www.zyte.com/blog/sowa-barrier-javascript/)** — 40.6% of top sites return unreadable content to a plain HTTP fetch because of client-side rendering. Relevant to any pipeline (yours included) that pulls source content via simple HTTP rather than a headless browser. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
