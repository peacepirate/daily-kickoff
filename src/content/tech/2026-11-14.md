## TL;DR

Apple shipped HomeView, its new smart-home hub with facial recognition — but Siri AI still trails Gemini and Alexa+ on it, so it's a doorbell/camera upgrade more than an assistant upgrade. Home Assistant shipped Connect AUX-2, a genuinely useful multi-protocol bridge for legacy gear. EV news was mostly Tesla tightening economics — Cybercab's robotaxi-zone-only launch and the Model Y AWD price floor rising to $50k — alongside real tire-wear data from InsideEVs worth reading before an EV purchase. The bigger signal this week: AI agents got direct access to money and bank data (ChatGPT Wallet, Gemini + Google Wallet), paired with an IEEE Spectrum piece on the compliance burden that creates for engineering teams — both land squarely in the regulated-fintech-manager lane. Two notable SaaS outages (Workday, Microsoft 365) are a vendor-concentration reminder, not urgent action.

## Tier 1 — Top Stories

- **[Apple details HomeView facial recognition, repair options, and more in new support guides](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/13/apple-details-homeview-facial-recognition-repair-options-and-more-in-new-support-guides/)** — Apple's new HomeView hub identifies household members by face to personalize what's shown; it needs an unobstructed, centered view of your face (no sunglasses, no angle tricks) to work reliably. So what: if you're weighing one for the house, placement and lighting matter more than usual — read this before you mount it. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Apple's Siri AI still lags behind Gemini and Alexa+ on Apple HomeView, the new Apple TV and iPhone](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/apple-intelligence/apples-new-siri-still-lags-behind-gemini-and-alexa-putting-its-entire-ecosystem-at-risk)** — Siri AI is better than old Siri, but the gap to Gemini and Alexa+ is still wide on Apple's newest hardware. So what: don't buy HomeView expecting an assistant upgrade — buy it for the camera/hub features and treat Siri as a bonus. [WAIT]
- **[No device left behind: Home Assistant Connect AUX-2](https://www.home-assistant.io/blog/2026/11/11/no-device-left-behind-home-assistant-connect-aux-2/)** — Nabu Casa's new box streams audio, drives RS-232 serial gear, adds a Z-Wave adapter, and acts as a Bluetooth hub — with PoE or USB power. So what: this is the first purpose-built way to pull old hi-fi or serial-controlled equipment into Home Assistant without another point hub. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Tesla Cybercab orders open at $29,990 + $299/mo, only in Robotaxi zones](https://electrek.co/2026/11/13/tesla-cybercab-personal-use-orders-299-subscription-geofence/)** *(via Electrek; InsideEVs covered the same news)* — Tesla is selling the Cybercab for personal use, but autonomy only works inside Tesla's existing Robotaxi geofences (Texas and Florida) and costs an extra $299/month subscription. So what: not purchasable-as-useful outside those two states right now — a data point on where robotaxi economics are heading, not a near-term option for you. [READ DEEPER]
- **[ChatGPT Wallet lets OpenAI's Dots agents make purchases for you](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1033425/openai-chatgpt-wallet)** — ChatGPT Pro users in the US can now link an Amex/Chase/Mastercard/Visa card so AI agents can check out on their behalf, with a biometric approval step at purchase time. So what: this is the first mainstream look at what "AI agent + payment card" controls look like in production — relevant to any conversation about agentic access to financial systems. [SHARE w/ leadership]
- **[How Microcredentials Help Engineers Build Trustworthy AI](https://spectrum.ieee.org/microcredentials-trustworthy-ai)** — Engineering teams are integrating AI faster than they can translate AI ethics, governance, and auditing into daily workflows, creating a compliance bottleneck. So what: this names the exact tension of adopting AI tooling across teams under regulatory oversight. [SHARE w/ leadership]

So what: if you're relying on HomePod minis, Siri AI support there depends entirely on a paired Apple Intelligence iPhone/iPad, not the speaker itself. [WAIT]

So what: worth a look if you're ever replacing a water heater and want it on the smart-home network. [READ DEEPER]
- **[FreeFlow Dictation](https://www.producthunt.com/products/freeflow-dictation)** — On-device Mac dictation with a built-in Claude Code voice hook. So what: directly useful for a Mac-based, Claude Code–using team. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Microsoft 365 Outage Resolving After Nearly 24 Hours](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/13/microsoft-365-outage-resolving/)** — A ~24-hour Microsoft 365 outage affected Outlook, Teams, Copilot, and Exchange-via-Apple-Mail before resolving.
