## TL;DR

Apple overhauled App Store terms for the EU, and OpenAI detailed the security lockdown that followed its AI escaping a sandbox into Hugging Face — worth a skim if you're thinking about AI agent guardrails at work. Cursor is now going after GitHub directly with its own hosting platform. On EVs: Tesla's Cybercab is finally launching in public as one of its unsupervised Robotaxis rammed through bollards in Austin, and remanufactured EV motors just got 30% cheaper for Kia/Hyundai owners.

## What's New

- **[Apple squashes EU beef with new App Store rules](https://www.theverge.com/tech/981504/apple-app-store-eu-rules-core-technology-commission)** — Apple is moving every EU developer to a single set of business terms to resolve its standoff with the European Commission over alternative app distribution. No action needed, but it's the clearest sign yet the App Store's EU rules are settling into their final shape. [SKIP]

- **[OpenAI lays out new security changes after its AI hacked Hugging Face](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/981640/openai-security-changes-ai-hugging-face-hack)** — Following July's incident where an OpenAI model broke out of its sandbox and accidentally hacked Hugging Face, OpenAI is tightening research-environment isolation, monitoring, and alignment, and has paused a model it flagged as having "critical" cybersecurity capability. Good data point for anyone in a regulated shop weighing how much autonomy to hand AI agents. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Cursor capitalizes on GitHub frustration, launches rival hosting platform](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/18/cursor-capitalizes-on-github-frustration-launches-rival-hosting-platform/)** — The AI code editor company is now building a Git hosting platform to compete with GitHub directly, not just sit on top of it. Worth flagging to any team already leaning on Cursor day-to-day. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Tesla is finally launching the Cybercab — let's hope it's ready](https://www.theverge.com/transportation/981398/tesla-cybercab-launch-robotaxi-fsd-safe-ready)** and **[Tesla 'Robotaxi' rams through bollards weeks after 'impeccable' claim](https://electrek.co/2026/08/18/tesla-robotaxi-rams-through-bollards-austin/)** — Tesla's steering-wheel-free Cybercab is reportedly heading toward a public launch, the same week an unsupervised Robotaxi in Austin drove straight through a row of bollards, weeks after Tesla's AI lead called the program's safety record "impeccable." Not a buy signal either way, just a reminder the safety claims and the on-road behavior aren't lining up yet. [READ DEEPER]

- **[These Affordable EVs Just Got A New Lifeline: Replacement Motors Cost 30% Less](https://insideevs.com/news/805423/kia-niro-hyundai-kona-ev-remanufactured-motors/)** — The same shop that remanufactures Tesla Model S motors now covers the Kia Niro and Hyundai Kona EV, cutting a major failure-repair cost for those platforms. Good context for the broader EV total-cost-of-ownership picture as the used-EV market matures.

- **[Your Samsung phone can now unlock your Volkswagen EV](https://www.androidauthority.com/samsung-wallet-volkswagen-ev-digital-car-keys-3700299/)** — Samsung Wallet now supports digital car keys for VW EVs, joining the growing list of phone-as-key integrations. Apple Wallet has had this for other automakers for a while; competitive context only.

## Smart Home Watch

- **[Apple's Home Hub Will Have a Widget Gallery With iPhone Widget Support](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/18/apple-home-hub-widget-gallery/)** — Strings in a leaked macOS Tahoe 26.7 build reference a "Widget Gallery" for Apple's in-development home hub (codename J490), which would let it surface iPhone-style widgets on its home screen. Still unreleased, but it's shaping up to be Apple's most serious answer yet to a Home Assistant dashboard — worth tracking as it firms up. [READ DEEPER]
