## TL;DR
Quiet day for Apple hardware, but macOS Golden Gate keeps trimming Intel-era features if you're tracking Mac lifecycle. EV news dominates: VW is clearing ID.4 inventory ahead of a redesign while delaying the ID.Buzz again to MY2028, and driver-assist safety is under fresh scrutiny — NHTSA opened a probe into comma.ai's openpilot after fatal crashes, and Tesla FSD was clocked speeding in a pre-vote EU test.

## What's New
- **[What's Gone in macOS Golden Gate and What's Next in macOS 28](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/24/macos-golden-gate-features-removed/)** — Golden Gate is the first macOS that only runs on Apple silicon; Intel Macs are capped at Tahoe with security updates through fall 2028, and Boot Camp Assistant is gone with it. No action if you're already on Apple silicon, but worth knowing if you're still running an Intel Mac.
- **[TestFlight App Gets New iOS 27 Features](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/24/testflight-ios-27-update/)** — Adds landscape support ahead of the iPhone Duo's October launch and lets TestFlight resize during iPhone Mirroring on a Mac. Minor, but the iPhone Duo reference is the first concrete signal of Apple's October hardware slate.
- **[The Volkswagen ID.4 Just Got Up to $12,500 Cheaper in America](https://insideevs.com/news/809539/volkswagen-id4-big-discounts-us/)** — Current-gen ID.4 is being cleared out ahead of a redesign. Real value if you want one now; a next-gen version is coming if you can wait. [BUY NOW]
- **[Volkswagen Delays The ID. Buzz's Return To The U.S. Again](https://electrek.co/2026/09/24/volkswagen-delays-electric-minibus-us-again/)** — Pushed from the 2027 to the 2028 model year, after already skipping 2026. Third delay in a row. [WAIT]
- **[NHTSA investigates comma.ai's openpilot over 5 crashes that killed 3](https://electrek.co/2026/09/24/comma-ai-openpilot-nhtsa-investigation-crashes/)** — Formal probe into ~30,000 aftermarket driver-assist devices after cars running openpilot hit stopped or slow-moving vehicles in their own lane. Relevant if you're weighing how much to trust any ADAS system, factory or aftermarket. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Tesla 'FSD' sped in 55% of Brussels 30 km/h zones ahead of EU vote](https://electrek.co/2026/09/24/tesla-fsd-belgium-test-speeding-30-kmh-eu-vote/)** — Independent test found FSD averaging 44 km/h in 30 km/h zones, days before EU member states vote on approving it bloc-wide. Same speeding issue Sweden and France already flagged. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Qualcomm's new 'Elite' sound chip might finally deliver the Wi-Fi earbud dream](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1000443/qualcomms-new-elite-sound-chip-might-finally-deliver-the-wi-fi-earbud-dream)** — Snapdragon Sound Elite Gen 2 integrates micro-power Wi-Fi 6E directly into the chip, aimed at lossless streaming that doesn't cut out when your phone is out of Bluetooth range. Announcement only — no products yet. [WAIT]
