## TL;DR

Apple's biggest story this week wasn't a product — Tim Cook is reflecting on his legacy in his final weeks as CEO, and a critical, actively-exploited macOS Screen Sharing vulnerability that lets attackers log in remotely without a password needs patching on every Mac you touch, today. Supply chain pressure kept building: the Commerce Secretary pushed back on Apple sourcing memory chips from China, which likely means the RAM and storage price crunch hitting laptops continues into fall. Smart home was quiet but healthy — the ESPHome Starter Kit shipped and SmartHomeScene published solid Zigbee/Thread reviews (Tuya ZRF01, Aqara Dimmer H2) — nothing urgent, just good reference material for your next build. EV and mobility news was mostly informational rather than actionable: Kia's EV3 now starts under $31K, GM's 2027 lineup gets Tesla Supercharger ports built in, and Waymo now covers 18 California counties, none of which changes anything you own today. On the productivity side, ChatGPT picked up Google Drive editing and a Recall-like activity memory, Pocket is shutting down (export your saves by October 8 if you use it), and Thrive's Joshua Kushner published a useful warning to leadership about letting AI enthusiasm override investment discipline.

## Tier 1 — Top Stories

- **[Vulnerability giving attackers full control of Macs is under active exploitation](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/08/vulnerability-giving-attackers-full-control-of-macs-is-under-active-exploitation/)** — A Screen Sharing bug lets remote attackers log into a Mac without a password, and it's already being exploited in the wild. Patch this on every Mac you and your team touch this week — this is not a wait-and-see item. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Tim Cook Reflects on His Legacy in Final Weeks as Apple CEO](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/14/apple-ceo-tim-cook-reflects-on-legacy-apple/)** — In a CBS News interview, Cook discussed how he wants to be remembered as he prepares to leave the CEO role. Worth reading regardless of Apple loyalty — a rare, candid reflection on leadership and succession from someone who ran a company this size for over a decade. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Should You Buy an iPhone 17 Now or Wait for iPhone 18?](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/14/iphone-17-vs-iphone-18-buying/)** — Apple isn't releasing a standard iPhone 18 this fall — only the 18 Pro, Pro Max, and a foldable Ultra ship in September, with the standard model delayed to spring 2027. If an upgrade is on your radar, prices are rumored to rise this fall, so there's little reason to wait. [BUY NOW]

- **[Commerce Secretary says US opposes Apple buying Chinese memory chips](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/14/commerce-secretary-says-us-opposes-apple-buying-chinese-memory-chips/)** — The US Commerce Secretary is pushing Apple away from Chinese memory suppliers as it looks for relief from the ongoing global RAM and storage shortage. Read: the laptop and phone price crunch you've been seeing isn't resolving soon.

- **[Waymo wins California approval to expand robotaxis across 18 counties](https://electrek.co/2026/08/14/waymo-cpuc-approval-california-expansion-18-counties/)** — California regulators cleared Waymo to charge for fully driverless rides across the whole Bay Area and LA, plus new markets in Sacramento and San Diego. The clearest signal yet that robotaxis are moving from pilot to default transportation option in major metros.

- **[Get ready to start building: The ESPHome Starter Kit is here](https://esphome.io/blog/2026/08/12/the-esphome-starter-kit-is-here/)** — ESPHome's first official hardware kit shipped, a solder-free way to build custom Home Assistant sensors and controls. If you've wanted to move past off-the-shelf Zigbee devices, this is the easiest on-ramp yet.

- **[Kia's EV3 will start under $31,000 in the US](https://www.engadget.com/2237429/kia-ev3-will-start-under-31-000-in-the-us/)** — Kia's cheapest EV undercuts most of the mainstream EV field on price. Not a purchase decision for you, but a useful data point on where affordable EV pricing is heading.

## Tier 2 — Category Signals
