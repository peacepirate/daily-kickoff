# Tech & Gadgets — Friday, October 23, 2026
*Coverage window: previous 24 hours*

## TL;DR
iPhone Duo officially launched today — $1,999, heavy pre-order backlog, and no reason to jump on a first-gen foldable before reviews settle. Ford's 2027 Mach-E gets more range standard at no extra cost, and China just stood up a 300-mile-in-5-minutes EV charger. On the work side, a new tool pauses runaway Claude Code/Codex agents, and a cert-authority meltdown plus an IEEE Spectrum piece on AI and engineering teams are both worth a closer look.

## What's New

- **[iPhone Duo launches today, demand "exceeding expectations"](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/23/iphone-duo-demand-exceeds-expectations/)** — Apple's first foldable shipped today at $1,999, and pre-order backlogs have already pushed new-order ship dates to January. First-gen foldable hardware and pricing — worth watching durability reviews before considering one. [WAIT]

- **[Mac gains share as broader PC market drops 21% on RAM price hikes](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/23/macbook-neo-helps-apple-outpace-rivals-as-market-declines/)** — Counterpoint says Apple grew Mac shipment share to 9.8% even as total PC shipments hit a multi-quarter low, driven by surging memory prices. If you're budgeting laptop refreshes, expect prices to keep climbing industry-wide, not just on Apple gear.

- **[2027 Ford Mustang Mach-E gets more range, standard, at no extra cost](https://insideevs.com/news/812887/2027-ford-mustang-mach-e-trail-battery-range/)** — The extended-range battery is now fitted across the lineup by default, plus a new lifted Trail trim. A meaningful upgrade if you're cross-shopping mainstream EVs.

- **[China gets a 300-mile-in-5-minutes EV charger](https://electrek.co/2026/10/22/another-megawatt-level-charger-enters-china-300-miles-in-5-minutes/)** — Chery's new 1.44-megawatt charging system claims 500km (311 miles) added in 5 minutes, ahead of anything available in the US. Charging speed like this is likely 2–3 years from reaching the US market, but it marks where EV infrastructure is headed.

- **[Peras pauses runaway Claude Code / Codex sessions before they burn your week](https://www.producthunt.com/products/peras)** — New Product Hunt launch built to catch AI coding agents that spiral into unplanned work or cost. Worth a look if any of your teams run agentic coding tools unsupervised. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Certificate authority eMudhra exits the TLS business after a botched signing ceremony](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/disgraced-emudhra-drops-out-of-tls-business-following-certificate-debacle/)** — A flubbed key-signing "ceremony" let anyone mint valid web certificates for any site; rather than fix it, eMudhra is walking away from issuing TLS certs entirely. A concrete reminder of how fragile the web PKI trust chain is. [READ DEEPER]

- **[IEEE Spectrum: "Why AI Can't Replace Engineering Teams"](https://spectrum.ieee.org/engineering-jobs-that-ai-cant-replace)** — Pushes back on the "ChatGPT answered that in five seconds" pressure engineers are facing, arguing the real value is judgment AI can't replicate. Useful ammunition for conversations with leadership about how you're deploying AI on your teams, not just whether you are. [SHARE w/ leadership]
