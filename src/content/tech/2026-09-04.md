## What's New
- **[Tesla's Cybercab Is Finally Giving Paid Robotaxi Rides. Here's What It Can And Can't Do](https://insideevs.com/news/807100/tesla-cybercab-robotaxi-public-rides-austin-official/)** — The two-seat, steering-wheel-free Cybercab began paid rides in Austin, with a redesigned brake system that skips brake fluid entirely. First real public test of Tesla's no-manual-controls bet — worth watching how liability and insurance shake out before it expands. [READ DEEPER]

- **[How Much Range The Tesla Model 3 And Model Y Lose After Almost 100,000 Miles](https://insideevs.com/news/807052/tesla-high-mileage-battery-health/)** — A study of roughly half a million EVs put Tesla near the bottom of the pack for battery longevity. If you're EV-shopping, longevity data like this is now a real differentiator, not just day-one range. [READ DEEPER]

- **[M6 vs. M5 Pro Mac Mini Buyer's Guide](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/m6-vs-m5-pro-mac-mini/)** — Apple's new Mac mini splits like last year's MacBook Pro: the cheaper M6 ($899, 12-core CPU, 16GB) is the newer chip, while the pricier M5 Pro ($1,699) carries over silicon from the current MacBook Pro. For most single-user workloads, the M6 base is the better buy. [BUY NOW]

- **[IONNA knocks nearly 50% off EV fast charging for Labor Day](https://electrek.co/2026/09/03/ionna-knocks-nearly-50-off-ev-fast-charging-for-labor-day/)** — Network-wide charging drops to $0.20/kWh from Sept 4–7. Worth queuing up if you're driving this weekend — the window closes Monday. [BUY NOW]

- **[Apple Hit With $2.7 Billion Lawsuit Over App Tracking Rules](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/03/apple-hit-with-lawsuit-over-app-tracking/)** — A London tribunal case alleges Apple imposes stricter App Tracking Transparency rules on third-party developers than on its own advertising business. Another data point on platform/antitrust pressure on Apple, relevant if your org tracks mobile platform risk. [SHARE w/ leadership]

## Smart Home Watch
- **[Aqara goes all in on smart lighting](https://www.theverge.com/tech/989581/aqara-smart-lighting-ifa-2026)** — Aqara's IFA lineup, including a pole-style Floor Lamp T1, supports both Zigbee and Thread. Another vendor betting on dual-protocol support rather than picking one — a good sign for interoperability in a Home Assistant setup.

- **[Apple Is Testing a Four-HomePod Surround System, Code Suggests](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/04/apple-testing-four-homepod-surround-system/)** — audioOS 27 beta code points to a four-speaker surround config with automatic room calibration and a new "Party Mode." If you're already in the HomePod ecosystem, worth waiting for before buying more speakers. [WAIT]

- **[This NAS company wants to run your local smart home](https://www.theverge.com/tech/990006/this-nas-company-wants-to-run-your-local-smart-home)** — Ugreen launched HomeAgent, a local platform combining camera storage, on-device AI, and device control in one hub. A local-first alternative worth a look if you ever want a Home Assistant alternative with integrated NAS storage — though unproven and closed. [READ DEEPER]
