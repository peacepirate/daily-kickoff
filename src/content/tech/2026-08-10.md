# Tech & Gadgets — Monday, August 10, 2026
*Coverage window: previous 24 hours*

## TL;DR
Apple's rumor mill is pointing at a bigger Watch overhaul — round screen, a return of the ceramic case, even a screenless fitness tracker — alongside October refreshes for MacBook Pro and iMac. If you're due for a Mac, that's a reason to wait a couple months. Anthropic quietly made Claude Code's auto mode the default, which is worth a heads-up to your teams. Everything else today is minor.

## What's New
- **[Apple Watch Rethink Could See Debut of Round Model, Screenless Fitness Tracker, and More](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/09/apple-watch-rethink/)** — Gurman reports Apple's industrial design team has spent a year on a broader Watch rethink, including a round-face model, a possible return of the ceramic case, and a screenless fitness band to compete with the Oura Ring. Nothing ships soon, but it signals the Watch line isn't static. [READ DEEPER]

- **[New MacBook Pro and Refreshed iMacs Likely Coming in October](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/10/new-macbook-pro-refreshed-imacs-october/)** — Apple is expected to refresh the base 14" MacBook Pro with an M6 chip and update the iMac line in October, with higher-end OLED MacBook Pros (M5 Pro/Max) following late this year or early 2027. If a Mac purchase is on your list, this is a reason to hold. [WAIT]

- **[Anthropic is turning Claude Code's auto mode on by default](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/09/anthropic-is-turning-claude-codes-auto-mode-on-by-default/)** — Auto mode reduces how often Claude Code stops for human approval during a session. Worth knowing before your ICs hit it unexpectedly on a live task. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[OpenAI slows down Astra development due to cybersecurity concerns](https://www.engadget.com/2233237/openai-slows-down-astra-development-due-to-cybersecurity-concerns/)** — Development on the Astra model reportedly slowed after the team couldn't rule out "critical cyber capabilities." A useful data point on how a frontier lab handles a model it isn't confident is safe to ship. [SHARE w/ leadership]

- **[Smart's Smallest EV Is Back, And It Promises A Lot More Range](https://insideevs.com/news/804340/smart-2-official-ev/)** — The original Smart EQ Fortwo topped out at 58 miles EPA range; the new #2 promises roughly triple that. Not confirmed for the US yet, but a signal the ultra-small-EV segment isn't dead.

- **[9 Google Pixel announcements every fan needs to watch for on August 12](https://www.androidauthority.com/big-google-pixel-announcements-august-12-2026-3694960/)** — Google's Pixel event lands August 12; noting for context since you track Android competitively. [SKIP]
