## TL;DR

Apple confirmed two launch events this month — an Oct 13 event centered on smart home hardware and an Oct 27 reveal of its first touchscreen MacBook Pro and OLED iPad mini — while reports suggest soft iPhone 18 Pro demand. VW is recalling ID.4s over a charging fire risk just as it reveals the ID.4's replacement, the ID. Tiguan, and Tesla is building out Megacharger stops for semis. Two free tools worth a look: TextExpander's new free tier and Anthropic's free OSS security scanner for open-source projects.

## What's New

- **[Apple's October 13 Launch: What's Coming](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/08/apple-october-13-launch/)** — Apple confirmed an October 13 event in New York — press hands-on time rather than a traditional keynote — expected to unveil a new smart home hub, a revamped Apple TV 4K, and a refreshed HomePod mini. If Apple's building a real hub for its smart home push, it's worth checking against your Home Assistant/Matter setup before buying anything else in that category this year. [WAIT]

- **[Apple to Launch Touchscreen OLED MacBook Pro and OLED iPad mini on October 27](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/08/apple-october-27-macbook-pro/)** — Bloomberg reports Apple's second October event lands around the 27th, with its first-ever touchscreen MacBook Pro, an OLED iPad mini, and M6 14-inch MacBook Pro/iMac refreshes. If you're due for a Mac upgrade, this is the generation to wait for, not next week's event. [WAIT]

- **[Report: Apple cuts iPhone 18 Pro series component orders amid weak demand](https://www.gsmarena.com/apple_reportedly_cuts_iphone_18_pro_series_component_orders_amid_weak_demand-news-74971.php)** — Apple reportedly cut iPhone 18 Pro/Pro Max component orders by up to 15% for October, citing weaker demand than last year's models. That's a supply/demand signal, not a defect — no rush, and watch for promos later this fall if you're eyeing one. [WAIT]

- **[VW ID.4 Recalled Over Fire Risk, Owners Told Not to Charge Past 80%](https://insideevs.com/news/811244/vw-id4-battery-fire-recall/)** — A battery issue in certain ID.4s can cause a fire while charging above 80%; VW is telling owners to cap charging until a fix ships. Not an Apple-ecosystem issue, but a real safety recall on a mainstream EV worth knowing about.

- **[The Volkswagen ID. Tiguan Looks Like a Big Upgrade Over the ID.4](https://insideevs.com/news/811254/vw-id-tiguan-launch-id4/)** — VW's ID.4 replacement brings back physical buttons and real door handles, a direct response to owner complaints about touchscreen-only controls on the outgoing car. A useful data point on EV makers walking back the all-touchscreen interior trend.

- **[Tesla Is Building Megachargers for Semis Where Truckers Already Stop](https://insideevs.com/news/811284/pilot-tesla-megacharger-rollout/)** — Tesla's first Pilot-hosted Megacharger station for semis is open in Georgia, with a dozen more under construction, placed at truck stops drivers already use. An early, concrete signal on how fast heavy-duty EV charging infrastructure is actually getting built rather than just announced.

- **[You can now use TextExpander for free across Mac, iPhone, iPad, and more](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/08/you-can-now-use-textexpander-for-free-across-mac-iphone-ipad-and-more/)** — TextExpander dropped its paywall; the snippet/keyboard-shortcut tool now works free on Mac, iPhone, iPad, Windows, and Chrome, keeping its core Snippets feature. Worth a nudge to any of your ICs still copy-pasting the same boilerplate every day. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Anthropic launches free AI security scans for open-source projects](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1008521/anthropic-open-source-oss-scanner)** — Anthropic's new OSS Scanner offers free, recurring security scans for open-source projects that opt in. If any of your teams maintain or lean heavily on open-source libraries, it's a no-cost extra set of eyes on vulnerabilities — though it means giving Anthropic's models scan access to that code. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
