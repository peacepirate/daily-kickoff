## TL;DR
Apple's new touchscreen MacBook Pro and OLED iPad mini are now shipping/pre-order — nothing here says jump on gen-1. The bigger EV story is infrastructure, not launches: Toyota/Waymo will build factory robotaxis on the bZ4X, Rivian's van got its first real range upgrade since 2021, and GM's $220M data-privacy settlement sets a new bar for driving-data consent. One small productivity win: Gemini now edits its own generated files in-chat.

## What's New

- **[Apple's Touchscreen MacBook Pro Could Ship 1.5M Units in 2027](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/30/oled-macbook-pro-1-5m-shipments-2027/)** — Pre-orders are open now for the first OLED touchscreen MacBook Pro ($2,899/$3,399), shipping Nov 6; TrendForce's modest 2027 volume forecast (1.2–1.5M units) suggests even Apple expects slow first-gen adoption. The new iPad mini shipping alongside it skips ProMotion (stuck at 60Hz) to protect the Pro's exclusivity. [WAIT]

- **[Toyota Is Turning The bZ EV Into A Waymo Robotaxi, Starting in 2027](https://insideevs.com/news/813848/toyota-bz-waymo-robotaxi-2027/)** — Waymo will build factory-spec driverless Toyota bZ4X robotaxis for Japan and the UK, starting 2027 — the clearest signal yet that OEM-built autonomy, not retrofit fleets, is how robotaxis scale internationally. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Rivian's Amazon Van Got A Big Upgrade To Cover More Routes](https://electrek.co/2026/10/29/rivian-electric-van-awd-30-percent-more-range/)** — Rivian's Amazon delivery van just got its first real spec bump since 2021 — a bigger LFP pack for 30% more range plus a new AWD variant — while the R2 crossover became Rivian's best-selling vehicle this quarter, prompting another delivery-guidance raise.

- **[GM to pay $220 million to resolve claims it sold driving habits data](https://www.theverge.com/transportation/1022555/gm-data-privacy-attorneys-general-lawsuit)** — GM settled with 36 state AGs over its Smart Driver program selling driving and location data to brokers; it must now get "express consent" before collecting that data. A preview of where consent rules for connected-device telemetry are headed. [SHARE w/ leadership]

- **[Gemini now lets you edit the files it creates right in the chat](https://www.gsmarena.com/gemini_now_lets_you_edit_the_files_it_creates_right_in_the_chat-news-75277.php)** — Docs, sheets, presentations, and code generated by Gemini can now be previewed and edited inline, no export/re-import loop required. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Faylee](https://www.producthunt.com/products/faylee)** — New tool: send a bug report link and your coding agent gets what it needs to act on it directly. Worth a look if your teams already run agentic coding workflows. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

## Smart Home Watch

- **[Ecowitt WH51 vs WH52 Soil Sensor Comparison](https://smarthomescene.com/reviews/ecowitt-wh51-vs-wh52-soil-sensor-comparison/)** — If you're running Ecowitt soil sensors in Home Assistant, the new WH52 adds temperature and conductivity readings on top of moisture — a direct upgrade path from the WH51.
