## TL;DR

This week was mostly Black Friday — real discounts on Apple hardware (MacBooks, AirPods, Watch Series 12) and on EVs (Kia, Lucid, and VW cutting $10k+), plus smart-home deals across 1,000+ devices. The one genuine Home Assistant signal was Aeotec joining Works with Home Assistant with nine certified Z-Wave devices. Apple's new Siri Recap (watchOS 27.3 beta) is privacy-forward but too brief and speaker-blind to be useful yet — a feature to revisit later, not use now. Workbrew 2.0 is worth a look if shadow IT on your Mac fleet is a live concern, and Rivian R2 driving impressions plus Harbinger's $240M raise and FedEx/Army orders were the only substantive non-deal EV stories. Otherwise a quiet week for real product news under the Black Friday noise.

- **[Aeotec joins Works with Home Assistant](https://www.home-assistant.io/blog/2026/11/18/aeotec-joins-works-with-home-assistant/)** — Aeotec brings 9 certified Z-Wave devices into the program, including new Z-Wave Long Range gear from its 8 series. So what: one more established Z-Wave brand with first-party HA certification — worth checking before your next sensor or siren purchase. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Weekend Black Friday Apple deals: MacBooks from $649, Series 12, AirPods $130 off, new iPad mini, more](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/21/official-black-friday-apple-deals-now-live/)** — All-time-low pricing across the Mac, Watch, AirPods, and the new OLED iPad mini lines. So what: if anything Apple is on your list, this week is the window. [BUY NOW]
- **[Kia, Lucid, and Volkswagen are slashing EV prices by $10,000+ for Black Friday](https://electrek.co/2026/11/20/kia-lucid-vw-slash-ev-prices-by-10000-black-friday/)** — Multiple brands are discounting EVs well beyond typical seasonal moves. So what: a genuine buy-now signal if you've been waiting on EV pricing to soften. [BUY NOW]
- **[Siri Recap Hands-On: Private, But Not Very Useful](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/20/siri-recap-hands-on/)** — The watchOS 27.3 beta feature summarizes nearby conversations with audio discarded after processing — but overly brief summaries and no speaker labels make it unreliable after two weeks of testing. So what: the privacy design is sound, the feature isn't ready — don't plan around it yet. [WAIT]
- **[iOS 27 lets you use two iPhones with the same phone number](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/20/ios-27-lets-you-use-two-iphones-with-the-same-phone-number/)** — iPhone Handoff now lets you keep one phone number active on two iPhones at once. So what: useful if you ever run a second iPhone (travel, testing, backup device) and don't want a second number.
- **[Apple @ Work: Workbrew 2.0 is an inventory for the development software your employees installed without asking](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/21/apple-work/)** — Workbrew 2.0 gives Mac admins visibility into developer tools installed outside sanctioned channels.
