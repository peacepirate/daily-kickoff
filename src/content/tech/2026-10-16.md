## TL;DR
Apple's iPhone Duo (first folding iPhone, $1,999) opened pre-orders this morning, and a chip-roadmap leak confirms the OLED MacBook Pro won't see new silicon until late 2027 — both are hold-off signals, not buy signals. Withings quietly started charging new users $100/year just to sync health data to Apple Health, a notable precedent for paywalling basic interoperability. Everything else today was noise: Tesla's flying Roadster stunt dominated headlines but has no bearing on a real purchase decision.

## What's New

- **[Apple Store Down Ahead of iPhone Duo Pre-Orders](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/16/apple-store-down-ahead-of-iphone-duo-pre-orders/)** — Pre-orders for Apple's first folding iPhone ($1,999) opened at 5 AM PT today. No durability or longevity data exists yet on a folding iPhone — if you want one, this isn't the generation to buy it on day one. [WAIT]

- **[OLED MacBook Pro Won't Get M7 Chips Until Late 2027](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/15/macbook-pro-m7-late-2027/)** — Gurman confirms Apple is skipping M6 Pro/Max/Ultra entirely; the next MacBook Pro chip bump is M7, not expected before late 2027. If you're on the fence about a Mac refresh, there's no faster chip coming to wait for in the near term. [WAIT]

- **[Withings wants new users to pay $100 a year to sync with Apple Health](https://www.engadget.com/2288346/withings-wants-new-users-to-pay-100-a-year-to-sync-with-apple-health/)** — New Withings accounts created after October 13 must pay for a "Plus" subscription just to sync data to Apple Health or Google Health Connect, functionality that used to be free. It's a clean, citable example of a vendor gating basic data portability behind a subscription. [SHARE w/ leadership]

- **[ChatGPT can peek at your Chrome tabs now](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1012510/openai-chatgpt-google-chrome-tabs)** — OpenAI's Chrome extension can now read your open tabs when you tag Chrome in a ChatGPT prompt, no copy-paste required. Small but genuinely useful for research-heavy workflows. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Gmail's Live AI search feature arrives for Google AI subscribers](https://www.engadget.com/2288545/gmail-live-ai-search-feature-arrives-for-google-ai-subscribers/)** — Gmail search gets a natural-language AI upgrade, but it's gated to Google AI plan subscribers. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Volvo Taps Nvidia To Bring 'Eyes-Off And Mind-Off' Driving To Its Cars](https://insideevs.com/news/812200/volvo-nvidia-hands-free-eyes-off-automated-driving/)** — Volvo says future models will handle point-to-point, hands-free automated driving using Nvidia compute. A mainstream automaker (not a robotaxi startup) committing to eyes-off driving is a real signal for where EV software is headed over the next few years. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Parallax](https://www.producthunt.com/products/parallax-5) / [Harness by Autonomous](https://www.producthunt.com/products/harness-by-autonomous)** — Two new tools launched today for managing multiple AI coding agents across machines from one place. Worth a glance if your teams are scaling up agentic coding tools. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Tesla reveals SpaceX-powered Roadster that flies and falls with style](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/15/tesla-reveals-spacex-powered-roadster-that-flies-and-falls-with-style/)** — Tesla's next-gen Roadster did a tethered flip using cold-gas thrusters at a Texas event. No price, no production date, no road-legal confirmation. Dominated every outlet today; changes nothing about any real EV purchase decision. [SKIP]
