## What's New

- **[1Password breach: what was accessed and what wasn't](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/25/1password-confirms-security-incident-heres-what-you-need-to-know/)** — An attacker compromised an employee's GitHub account, used it to push malicious code to backend services, and pulled customer names and emails. No vault contents (passwords) were touched. If you're on 1Password, no password reset needed — just watch for phishing using your email. [READ DEEPER]

- **[AirPods Pro 3 drop to $149 on Amazon](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/25/amazon-airpods-pro-3-deal-back/)** — Down from $249, a record low and one of the better Apple deals this Black Friday. [BUY NOW]

- **[Kia, Lucid, and VW slash EV prices $10,000+ for Black Friday](https://electrek.co/2026/11/25/kia-lucid-vw-slash-ev-prices-by-10000-black-friday/)** — Real discounts, not rebate games, across several mainstream and premium EVs. If the EV transition is on your radar, this is the kind of price move worth tracking. [BUY NOW]

- **[Redesigned OLED MacBook Air could launch in 2027 or 2028](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/25/oled-macbook-air-2027-2028/)** — Gurman reports the next MacBook Air refresh (early 2027, M6 chips) keeps the current design; an OLED overhaul is a year or two further out. No reason to delay a Mac purchase waiting for this. [WAIT]

- **[WhatsApp's scheduled messages are rolling out globally](https://www.gsmarena.com/whatsapp_scheduled_messages_are_now_rolling_out_globally-news-75645.php)** — Press and hold send, pick a time 10 minutes to 14 days out. Works offline too. Minor but genuinely useful.

- **[Wispr Flow improves dictation accuracy with new Canto-2 model](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/25/wispr-flow-improves-dictation-accuracy-with-new-canto-2-speech-to-text-model/)** — Fewer transcription errors, better handling of names and technical terms, rolling out to Mac and iPhone. Worth a look if anyone on your team dictates notes or docs. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Toyota set to reveal its first extended-range EV](https://electrek.co/2026/11/25/toyota-reveal-first-erev-looks-like-highlander/)** — Debuting Nov 27, reportedly based on the Highlander. EREVs (gas generator + EV drivetrain) are a notable middle path between hybrids and full EVs — one to watch as more mainstream brands enter.

- **[Ionna slashes EV charging prices for Thanksgiving](https://insideevs.com/news/816844/ionna-charging-price-discount-thanksgiving-2026/)** — The newer charging network (backed by major automakers) is halving prices network-wide for the holiday travel window. Infrastructure pricing pressure is a good sign for EV charging economics generally.
