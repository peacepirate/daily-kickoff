## TL;DR

Apple's M6 MacBook Pro review landed, and the verdict is: fine, but the base model is missing whatever makes the Pro/Max tiers worth it — a signal to wait if you're due for a refresh. Adobe went all-in on agentic AI and MCP support across Photoshop, Premiere, and Firefly, the one item today with real engineering-tooling relevance. EV news was mostly design and infrastructure chatter — Polestar's concept car, a PlugShare redesign, and Honda pulling back from EVs in favor of a $3B hybrid bet.

## What's New

- **[Review: Apple's M6 MacBook Pro is good, but it's missing all the exciting stuff](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/11/review-apples-1999-m6-macbook-pro-feels-stuck-in-the-middle/)** — Ars's usual take on the base 14" Pro: solid chip, but it lags the Pro/Max models where it counts, as usual. If you're buying for the team, this isn't the tier to get excited about. [WAIT]

- **[Adobe is going all in on agentic AI](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1030593/adobe-max-ai-agents-mcp-firefly-assistant-updates)** — Announced at Adobe Max: third-party AI agents will get MCP access to Photoshop, Premiere, and Firefly (full support in 2027), plus new auto-remove tools for objects and blemishes shipping now in Premiere/Photoshop/Lightroom. Worth tracking if your teams touch Creative Cloud workflows or build on MCP. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Apple Maps could factor parking into your arrival time](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/10/apple-maps-factor-parking-into-arrival-time/)** — A "TimeToPark" flag spotted in the iOS 27.3 beta would have Maps estimate time spent parking and walking, not just driving. Internal-only for now, no live feature yet.

- **[Meta's AI agent Muse lands on Apple Watch and Android Wear OS](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/metas-ai-agent-muse-lands-on-apple-watch-and-wear-os/)** — Voice calls, texts, or dictation now reach Meta's assistant directly from the watch. Another data point on how fast AI agents are spreading to wrist-worn hardware.

- **[Polestar's Next EVs Will Borrow From This Gorgeous New Concept](https://insideevs.com/news/814974/polestar-formula-2030-concept-reveal/)** — The Formula 2030 concept previews design language headed for the next Polestar 2 and the Polestar 7, including a return to physical buttons in the interior.

- **[PlugShare Gets A Major Redesign, Adds Dark Mode And iPhone Duo Support](https://insideevs.com/news/814963/plugshare-app-update-redesign-dark-mode-iphone-duo/)** — Near-total rebuild of the charger-finder app most EV drivers already use. Worth updating if you're on it.

- **[Honda Is Betting $3 Billion On Hybrids As It Pulls Back From EVs](https://insideevs.com/news/814945/honda-new-hybrid-car-factory-ohio-failed-ev-push/)** — New Ohio factory and hybrid lineup after Honda's EV ambitions fell short of plan. [READ DEEPER]
