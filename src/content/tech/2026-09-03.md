## What's New
- **[Anthropic upgrades Claude's computer use to run in the background on Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/02/anthropic-upgrades-claude-codes-computer-use-to-run-in-the-background-on-mac/)** — Claude on Mac can now drive your desktop without staying in the foreground window. If any of your teams use Claude Code's computer-use features, this removes a real friction point for hands-off automation. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[iOS 27 Introduces New 'iPhone Handoff' Feature](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/02/ios-27-iphone-handoff-feature/)** — A leaked demo shows iOS 27 letting you switch between two iPhones while keeping the same phone number active on each. Also in this release: three fixes to long-standing Apple Mail annoyances. Nothing to act on yet, but worth knowing before the iPhone 18 launch next week.

- **[Ford Wants To Sell 100,000 Fathom Electric Pickups In Year One. That's A Huge Goal](https://insideevs.com/news/806974/ford-fathom-ev-sales-plan/)** — Ford's $30K compact EV truck is targeting a first-year volume that would put it right behind Tesla. A useful gauge of whether mainstream EV demand is actually there, independent of the hype around any one launch.

- **[Hyundai opens IONIQ 3 EV orders, starting at about $30,000](https://electrek.co/2026/09/02/hyundai-opens-ioniq-3-ev-orders-starting-30000/)** — Hyundai's new entry-level EV hatch undercuts most of the competition on price. Another data point that the sub-$30K EV segment is filling in fast.

- **[$41M could bring 40 new EV fast-charging sites to Minnesota](https://electrek.co/2026/09/02/41-million-could-bring-40-new-ev-fast-charging-sites-to-minnesota/)** — Funding would push chargers beyond the state's three major interstates and into secondary routes. Not your state, but the pattern — fast-charging money moving off the interstate spine — is the thing to watch nationally.

- **[Google Pixel Watch 5 In-Depth Review: Is It Enough?](https://www.dcrainmaker.com/2026/09/google-watch-5-in-depth-review.html)** — DC Rainmaker's lab testing found one of the biggest GPS accuracy jumps they've measured on a smartwatch, despite no visible hardware change. Pixel, not Apple Watch, but a useful marker of how good GPS-in-a-watch has gotten.

## Smart Home Watch
- **[Anker's New MindBase Home Hub Runs AI Locally, Doubles as NAS](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/03/anker-mindbase-new-cameras/)** — MindBase works alongside HomeKit and Matter-enabled devices from other brands, runs an on-device AI agent, and doubles as a 48TB NAS for photos/files. It's Anker trying to become a Home Assistant-adjacent hub rather than just an accessory maker — worth watching if it gets real third-party Matter support. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Aqara Debuts Five Matter Smart Lights With Apple Home Support](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/03/aqara-matter-smart-lights/)** — All five support Thread and Zigbee in addition to Matter, and pair with Aqara's existing sensor/switch lineup. Direct fit for expanding smart lighting without locking into a single hub vendor. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Govee Debuts $400 Matter-Enabled Sky Ceiling Light](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/03/govee-sky-ceiling-light/)** — A "mood-responsive" ceiling light that changes color based on an AI reading of your mood. Matter-enabled, but a novelty at this price point. [SKIP]
