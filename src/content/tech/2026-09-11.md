## What's New

- **[Apple Watch Series 12 & Ultra 4 ship with always-listening audio features](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/10/apple-watch-siri-recap/)** — Siri Recap and Live Rewind continuously listen to conversations around the wearer and summarize or transcribe them. Apple says transcripts only save on-device when you act on them, and there's no way to trigger Live Rewind silently — a direct answer to the Meta-glasses comparisons already circulating. Ultra 4 also drops GLONASS support (keeps GPS, Galileo, QZSS, BeiDou), and a ceramic Series 12 now costs $599 to repair without AppleCare+, up from cheaper aluminum options. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Don't buy the 'regular' AirPods 5](https://www.engadget.com/2255399/dont-buy-the-regular-airpods-5/)** — Engadget's review: the first time you need to adjust volume without pulling out your phone, you'll wish you'd paid the extra $20 for the step-up model. [BUY NOW]

- **[EV sales are booming in Europe — and plunging in North America](https://electrek.co/2026/09/10/ev-sales-are-booming-in-europe-and-plunging-in-north-america/)** — Global EV sales barely moved in August, but that's two opposite stories: Europe up 36% year-over-year, North America down 33%. Not a demand recovery — a regional split.

- **[Tesla Cybertruck Powershare now backs up a home alongside a Powerwall 3](https://electrek.co/2026/09/10/tesla-cybertruck-powershare-powerwall-3-home-backup/)** — Running the truck and a Powerwall 3 together adds over three days of home backup power. Tesla promised this for 2025; it's landing almost two years late. Relevant if you ever weigh an EV as part of home backup power planning.

- **[Raycast 2.0 is out](https://www.producthunt.com/products/raycast)** — Next-generation release of the launcher/productivity tool. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Slack can now vibe-code interactive charts and reports inside chats](https://www.theverge.com/tech/989853/slackforce-surfaces-launch)** — New "Surfaces" feature: describe what you need to Slackbot, and it builds interactive dashboards, polls, or microsites live in the channel, pulling from connected apps like Google Drive or Salesforce. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Ford bids farewell to the F-150 Lightning](https://electrek.co/2026/09/10/ford-bids-farewell-f-150-lightning-fathom-pickup-nears/)** — Discontinued as Ford preps a $30,000 "Fathom" EV pickup and an extended-range (EREV) model for 2027. [SKIP]
