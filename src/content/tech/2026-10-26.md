## TL;DR
The iPhone Duo's inner screen is cracking at the hinge in a consistent pattern — iFixit's teardown and three other outlets independently corroborate it, so this isn't a one-off. Apple also heads into its biggest hardware week in years: OLED/touchscreen MacBook Pro and OLED iPad mini 8 both ship this week, while MacBook Air's OLED jump stays over a year out. One cert-security fix worth a heads-up to engineering, one AI-ROI data point worth a heads-up to leadership.

## What's New

- **[iFixit's look inside the iPhone Duo may explain those broken screens and hinges](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1018233/ifixit-iphone-duo-teardown-repairability-hinge)** — iFixit scored the Duo 5/10 for repairability (the best of any foldable so far) but its teardown points to the hinge mechanism as the likely cause of a defect: owners' inner screens are cracking in the same spot, along the hinge line, confirmed independently by 9to5Mac and GSMArena. If you or anyone on your team is eyeing one, this is a hold-off signal, not a rumor. [WAIT]

- **[Touchscreen OLED MacBook Pro launches this week](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/25/macbook-pro-redesign-oled-touch-launching-this-week/)** — Apple's first touchscreen Mac laptop, OLED display, lighter build — the biggest MacBook Pro redesign since 2021, expected to ship this week per Gurman. Expect a premium price. Don't buy the current model in the meantime. [WAIT]

- **[OLED iPad mini 8 debuting this week](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/25/ipad-mini-8-redesign-with-oled-coming-this-week/)** — First display and chip refresh since 2024, also landing this week alongside the MacBook Pro.

- **[OLED MacBook Air and iMac remain in testing](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/25/oled-macbook-air-and-imac-in-testing/)** — Still LCD for now. Gurman expects a new iMac this week (LCD) and a MacBook Air refresh in early 2027 (also LCD) — OLED on either has no firm timeline. If you were holding out for an OLED Air, keep waiting past next year's refresh. [WAIT]

- **[Apple addresses web security threat from compromised eMudhra certificates](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/25/apple-blocks-compromised-emudhra-certificates/)** — Weak private keys on eight eMudhra intermediate CA certs (trusted by Safari, Chrome, Edge, Firefox) could have let attackers impersonate sites. No evidence of exploitation, but Apple's blocked the certs and has a macOS update with an additional fix coming. Worth a heads-up to your security-conscious engineers. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[AI delivers 'zero financial payoff' to most companies, Apollo economist says](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/25/ai-delivers-zero-financial-payoff-to-most-companies-according-to-apollo-economist/)** — Despite massive AI investment, an Apollo Global economist finds no measurable financial payoff for corporate America outside the tech sector itself. Useful counterweight if your org is being pushed toward AI spend on vibes rather than measured ROI. [SHARE w/ leadership]

- **[Zen Browser is the Arc replacement I was looking for](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1018275/zen-browser-arc-replacement)** — With Arc dead and its acquirer Atlassian pivoting to the AI-first Dia browser, Zen Browser is a live open-source alternative that keeps Arc's workspace/sidebar model without the AI push. Worth a look if you miss Arc. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Slate's bare-bones electric truck surprised this early reviewer](https://insideevs.com/reviews/813164/slate-ev-truck-review-tedward/)** — An early drive of Slate's stripped-down, unfinished prototype EV truck found it more comfortable and capable than expected, rough edges aside. Early signal on where the budget EV truck segment is headed. [READ DEEPER]
