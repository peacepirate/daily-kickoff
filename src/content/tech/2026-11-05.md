## TL;DR
Apple's five-way hardware refresh — iPad mini, iMac, and three MacBook Pro configs including the first OLED touchscreen model — ships tomorrow. EV news is mostly about plumbing: GM is recalling 67,000 EVs for a brake software bug, the Silverado EV picks up a NACS port, and China's battery dominance is forcing a slow US supply-chain response. On the productivity side, Apple is reportedly cutting internal AI spend even as a new macOS tool for reviewing GitHub PRs with local Claude Code showed up on Product Hunt.

## What's New

- **[Apple Launching Five New Products Tomorrow](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/05/apple-launching-five-new-products-tomorrow/)** — iPad mini (OLED, A20 Pro, from $699), iMac (M6, from $1,499), and three MacBook Pro configs ship November 6, including the first OLED touchscreen 14"/16" models ($2,899/$3,399). The M6 iMac and standard 14" MacBook Pro are routine chip bumps; the touchscreen Pro is a first-gen product worth letting reviews settle before buying. [WAIT]
- **[Apple Is Reportedly Cutting Budgets for Its Internal AI Usage](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/04/apple-is-reportedly-cutting-budgets-for-its-internal-ai-usage/)** — Apple is said to be significantly scaling back what it spends on AI tooling for its own engineering teams. Useful data point if you're benchmarking AI tooling spend against a large peer org. [SHARE w/ leadership]
- **[CC Auto Review](https://www.producthunt.com/products/cc-auto-review)** — A macOS app that reviews GitHub PRs using your local Claude Code install instead of a separate API bill. Worth a look if any of your teams want automated first-pass PR review wired into their workflow. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
- **[GM Is Recalling More Than 67,000 Cadillac and Chevy EVs Over a Brake Software Defect](https://electrek.co/2026/11/04/gm-recalls-67000-cadillac-chevy-evs-over-brake-software-defect/)** — A software glitch can cause temporary loss of braking; the fix is a software update, not a return-to-dealer recall. Worth flagging if anyone you know owns an affected Cadillac or Chevy EV.
- **[The 2027 Chevy Silverado EV Gets a Power Bump and a NACS Port](https://insideevs.com/news/814480/2027-chevrolet-silverado-ev-more-power-nacs/)** — All GM EVs are moving to Tesla's NACS charging standard, and this one also gets more power. One more mainstream model that plugs into the same charging network as Tesla's.
- **[Ford Fathom EV Pickup Prices Will Range From $28,350 to the High $30Ks](https://electrek.co/2026/11/04/ford-fathom-ev-pickup-prices-range-from-28350-to-high-30ks/)** — Ford's upcoming electric pickup undercuts most of the EV market on price, starting under $30K. A useful data point for the EV affordability story.
- **[China Has an Iron Grip on EV Batteries. Here's America's Plan to Break It](https://insideevs.com/news/814448/us-2030-ev-battery-strategy-supply-chain/)** — The US wants a domestic battery supply chain by 2030, but tens of billions of dollars in battery projects have already been canceled. Explains why EV pricing and model availability keep swinging. [READ DEEPER]
