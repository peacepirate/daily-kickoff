## TL;DR
Apple pushed the iOS 27.3 / macOS 27.3 betas with real workflow features — Mail unsubscribe via Siri, native Markdown in Quick Look, Vertical Tabs in Safari — and the Apple Watch's contested AI audio features went live. On the EV side, IONIQ 5 sales and NEVI charging funding both climbed, and Mercedes is testing a battery architecture that charges cells individually. A Mac security tool worth a look if your teams are pasting into AI chat tools.

## What's New

- **[iOS 27.3 beta: what's new](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-27-3-beta-features/)** — Biggest iOS 27 update since launch: Safari gets Vertical Tabs and Adaptive Dark Mode, Siri can unsubscribe you from mailing lists and undo replies in Mail, and StandBy mode features expand to iPhone 11 and newer. Worth the update once it ships. [READ DEEPER]
- **[macOS 27.3 adds native Markdown rendering to Quick Look](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/03/macos-27-3-adds-native-markdown-rendering-to-quick-look/)** — Space-bar preview on a `.md` file now renders formatted text instead of raw text. Small but genuinely useful if you live in Markdown notes.
- **[Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4's AI audio features are going live](https://www.gsmarena.com/apple_watch_series_12_and_ultra_4s_controversial_ai_features_are_going_live-news-75336.php)** — Live Rewind (replay the last 15 seconds of speech as text) and Siri Recap are rolling out in the first watchOS 27.3 beta. If you're on a Series 12/Ultra 4, this is the feature Apple delayed at launch.
- **[PasteGuard for Mac](https://www.producthunt.com/products/pasteguard-2)** — Blocks secrets and personal data from being pasted into AI chat tools. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
- **[iPhone jailbreak gets past Lockdown Mode and survives an update](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/03/iphone-jailbreak-lockdown-mode/)** — Hacker team Calif jailbroke an iPhone 18 Pro with Lockdown Mode enabled, and the jailbreak persisted after an iOS update. Undercuts Apple's "extreme protection" pitch for the mode. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Hyundai's IONIQ 5 tops 33,000 sales in 2026](https://electrek.co/2026/11/03/hyundai-ioniq-5-finds-footing-2026-sales-top-33000/)** — October sales up 21%, making it one of the most popular non-Tesla EVs in the US. Durable demand signal, not a one-month blip.
- **[Pennsylvania adds another $37M in NEVI charging funds](https://electrek.co/2026/11/03/pennsylvania-is-way-ahead-on-nevi-charging-now-comes-another-37m/)** — 515 new public charging ports planned for the Philadelphia region, including curbside chargers. Infrastructure keeps expanding despite the on-and-off federal funding fights elsewhere.
- **[Mercedes wants to rethink EV batteries with cell-level micro-converters](https://insideevs.com/news/814348/mercedes-ev-battery-micro-converter-technology/)** — Instead of charging the pack as one unit, each cell charges or discharges independently. Early-stage, but could change both charge speed and failure isolation if it ships. [WAIT]
