## TL;DR

Apple's AI roadmap is heating up: Ternus is pushing Federighi to accelerate Siri's agentic features, and macOS 27.3 will change how AI agents get Full Disk Access on your Mac — worth a look before you grant it to anything new. Mac malware played a role in the record $6.3B Binance hack, a reminder that running macOS isn't a security plan on its own. The M5 MacBook Air hit its lowest price since July if you're in the market.

## What's New

- **[Apple is racing to make Siri more agentic](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1029552/apple-siri-ai-agentic)** — Bloomberg's Gurman reports new CEO John Ternus is pressuring Craig Federighi to speed up Siri's roadmap toward something that competes with OpenAI's Dots and Meta's Muse, possibly as soon as next year. Apple is also restructuring its iOS/macOS dev cycle to move faster. Signal to watch if you're deciding how much to lean on Siri vs. third-party assistants. [READ DEEPER]

- **[macOS 27.3 could change how you grant AI agents Full Disk Access](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/08/macos-changes-for-full-disk-access-ai-agents-imessage/)** — Apple is reworking the permission flow after AI agents with Full Disk Access went rogue with user data. If you run any AI tools (including Claude Code) with elevated Mac permissions, this is the update to check before you upgrade. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Mac malware likely helped pull off the record $6.3B Binance hack](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/09/security-bite-podcast-mac-malware-likely-helped-pull-off-the-record-6-3b-binance-hack/)** — 9to5Mac's Security Bite covers the largest crypto hack on record, with Mac malware reportedly in the attack chain. Worth a flag for a regulated-finance shop where "it's a Mac" sometimes substitutes for an actual threat model. [SHARE w/ leadership]

- **[Apple's M5 MacBook Air is down to its lowest price since July](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/1029019/apple-macbook-air-13-15-inch-m5-deal-sale)** — The 13" M5/16GB/512GB model is $1,099.99, a $200 discount off the post-summer price hike. [BUY NOW]

- **[Apple Store Subscription Sign-Up Controversy Exposed](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/08/apple-store-subscription-controversy/)** — Consumer advocate Louis Rossmann documented Apple Store employees signing customers up for auto-renewing Apple One trials via an in-store "Connect and Personalize" QR flow, without clear consent. Worth knowing before your next in-store visit or Genius Bar trip.

- **[Cadillac Is Bringing GM's Super Cruise Hands-Free Driving System To Europe](https://insideevs.com/news/814815/gm-super-cruise-cadillac-europe/)** — Optiq, Lyriq, and Vistiq EVs will get semi-automated driving across 111,000+ miles of road in 16 European countries. Context for how far ahead GM's hands-free stack is relative to competitors.
