## TL;DR
Apple's new MacBook Pro, OLED iPad mini, and M6 iMac start shipping today — worth waiting for independent reviews before buying. On the EV side, Tesla confirmed the Cybertruck's production wind-down and BMW extended its battery/infotainment update to the i7, while charging infrastructure keeps expanding. Google is retiring Firebase Studio in favor of its newer AI dev tools, worth flagging to your engineering teams.

## What's New

- **[OLED iPad Mini, M6 iMac, and New MacBook Pros Available From Today](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/06/ipad-mini-oled-and-m6-imac-available-today/)** — Apple's touchscreen MacBook Pro (M5 Pro/Max), OLED iPad mini, and M6 iMac are shipping and in stores today. No independent reviews yet — the touchscreen MacBook Pro in particular is a first-generation feature. [WAIT]

- **[Tesla Is Killing The Cybertruck To Make Room For More Robotaxis](https://insideevs.com/news/814618/tesla-cybertruck-production-ending-official/)** — Musk confirmed Cybertruck production in Texas winds down around mid-2027 to free up capacity for Cybercab. Signals where Tesla's actual production priorities are, regardless of truck demand.

- **[BMW Bumps Battery for the Better: The 2027 i7 60 xDrive, Driven](https://arstechnica.com/cars/2026/11/bmw-bumps-battery-for-the-better-the-2027-i7-60-xdrive-driven/)** — BMW's flagship EV sedan gets a larger battery and updated infotainment for 2027. A data point on how fast mainstream luxury EV range is moving, even if it's not your segment.

- **[Tesla Is Now Powering One Of Its Biggest Charging Rivals](https://insideevs.com/news/814582/tesla-supercharging-ev-go-chicago/)** — EVgo opened its first station with Tesla Superchargers mixed in, with hundreds more stalls planned. Charging networks are converging faster than brand rivalries would suggest.

- **[BMW Is Giving New EV Buyers $500 In Free Charging](https://insideevs.com/news/814588/bmw-giving-new-ev-buyers-500-free-charging/)** — $500 in credits for 2027 BMW EVs, including $200 usable on the IONNA network specifically.

- **[Google is ditching Firebase Studio soon, and it wants you to use its newer AI tools instead](https://www.engadget.com/2308541/google-is-ditching-firebase-studio-soon-and-it-wants-you-to-use-its-newer-ai-tools-instead/)** — Google is consolidating its AI dev tooling into Antigravity and Google AI Studio. If any team is on Firebase Studio, this is a forced migration, not optional. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Claude Gets Personal Finance Feature With Bank Account Linking](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/05/claude-personal-finance/)** — Anthropic added read-only bank/card linking to Claude for spend tracking, bill monitoring, and fee detection. No ability to move money.

## Smart Home Watch

- **[Apple's New HomeView Smart Home Hub: Here's How Much RAM It Has](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/05/apple-homeview-how-much-ram/)** — HomeView runs the A18 chip with 8GB RAM, the same silicon class as iPhone 16.
