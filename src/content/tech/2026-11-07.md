## TL;DR

Home Assistant shipped its biggest release ever this week — 2026.11 folds HACS directly into the core app as a new built-in Marketplace, worth reading before you update. Apple's redesigned OLED touchscreen MacBook Pro is now in customers' hands, the first MacBook Pro redesign in five years. On the security side, Chainalysis disclosed a multifront breach that used AI agents to steal every customer's credentials — a concrete example of the AI-agent attack surface worth raising in a regulated-industry security conversation. EV infrastructure kept improving: EVgo opened its first Tesla Superchargers and Toyota quietly added NACS fast-charging to every 2027 RAV4 PHEV trim. Raycast's new AI feature (voice dictation plus screen awareness) is a clean productivity pickup worth passing to your engineering team.

## Tier 1 — Top Stories

- **[2026.11: A lot in store!](https://www.home-assistant.io/blog/2026/11/04/release-202611/)** — Home Assistant's biggest release ever folds HACS directly into the core app as a new built-in Marketplace; everything you've already installed stays installed and entity IDs/automations keep working. Worth reading the full changelog before you update given the scope. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Hands-On With Apple's New OLED Touchscreen MacBook Pro](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/06/oled-macbook-pro-hands-on/)** — First MacBook Pro redesign in five years: thinner, lighter, OLED touch display, and the glowing Apple logo is back. If you're due for a Mac refresh, this is the generation to look at.
- **[Chainalysis says hackers stole credentials and tokens for all of its customers](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/11/chainalysis-says-hackers-stole-credentials-and-tokens-for-all-of-its-customers/)** — A "multifront cyberattack" used AI agents plus an on-path data intercept to pull every customer's credentials. A concrete example of an AI-agent attack vector, relevant to any regulated-industry security conversation. [SHARE w/ leadership]
- **[Raycast combines voice dictation and screen awareness in new AI feature](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/06/raycast-combines-voice-dictation-and-screen-awareness-in-new-ai-feature/)** — You can now talk to Raycast's AI about whatever's on your screen without re-explaining context. A direct productivity pickup for anyone already on Raycast. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
