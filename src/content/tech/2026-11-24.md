- **[Apple Watch Series 12 is cheaper than ever ahead of Black Friday](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/1034512/apple-watch-series-12-black-friday-deal-sale)** — The 42mm Wi-Fi+GPS model dropped to $299 ($100 off) at Amazon and Walmart, a new all-time low. If you've been holding off on a watch upgrade, this is the price to act on. [BUY NOW]

- **[MacBook Neo Sees Record Low $599 Price in Latest Black Friday Sale](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/23/macbook-neo-100-off/)** — The entry 256GB model is down to $599, the best price since Apple's summer increase. Citrus color only at this price; other configs are discounted less. [BUY NOW]

- **[Anthropic launches Claude Ship, letting AI agents review, fix, and approve code changes](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/anthropic-launches-claude-ship-letting-ai-agents-review-fix-and-approve-code-changes/)** — Positioned to clear the code-review bottleneck as AI-generated PRs pile up, by letting agents handle routine review and approval. Worth evaluating against your teams' current review load before you decide how much autonomy to grant it. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Google alternative Kagi launches an email service](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1041000/kagi-mail-email-google-gmail-alternative)** — Kagi Mail is in public beta: no ads, no surveillance, open standards, built by the same team behind Kagi Search and the Orion browser. Early days, but a credible Gmail alternative is rare enough to track. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Volkswagen Launches Mass-Market Vehicle-To-Grid Charging, Letting EV Owners Earn Money](https://www.motor1.com/news/816581/elli-bidi-charger-v2g-payments/)** — VW's Elli unit is shipping a European BiDi Charger that pays plug-in bonuses, with a Vehicle-to-Home system planned for 2027. Europe-only for now, but it's the clearest signal yet that V2G monetization is moving from pilot to product. [READ DEEPER]

- **[iOS 27.2 upgraded Apple Photos in two ways](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/23/ios-27-2-just-upgraded-apple-photos-in-two-ways-heres-whats-new/)** — Two incremental Photos improvements landed in the 27.2 update, on top of what iOS 27 already added.
