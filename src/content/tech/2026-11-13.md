## TL;DR

Apple shipped both halves of its home push today — HomeView and the new Apple TV 4K — both leaning on a new Siri AI. On the EV side, the bigger story is backup power: two separate home-charger announcements add the ability to run your house off your car or a home battery during an outage. Hybrid, not pure EV, is what's actually winning the mainstream SUV segment right now.

## What's New

- **[Apple Launches Two Products Today](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/13/apple-launches-two-products-today/)** — HomeView ($299, 7" display, Siri AI, recognizes up to six users by face) and the 4th-gen Apple TV 4K (A19 chip, Siri AI, new remote with Find My) both shipped today. Siri AI is also coming to 2nd- and 3rd-gen Apple TV 4K units via tvOS 27.2.1, so existing hardware isn't locked out. Worth watching how HomeView's Siri AI plays with your existing Home Assistant/Matter setup before buying — this is day-one hardware from a company with no smart-hub track record. [WAIT]

- **[America's 3 Most Popular SUVs Are About To Be Hybrid Or Electric](https://insideevs.com/news/815319/honda-crv-hybrid-toyota-rav4-tesla-model-y/)** — The Honda CR-V is going hybrid-only starting with the 2028 model year, joining the Toyota RAV4. Two of the best-selling vehicles in the US no longer offer a pure-gas option — a stronger signal on where mainstream buyers are actually going than any EV launch headline. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Apple Continues to Move Up iPhone Duo Delivery Dates](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/12/iphone-duo-delivery-dates-continue-to-move-up/)** — Some January 25 delivery estimates have moved up to mid-December. Only matters if you've already ordered one. [SKIP]

## Smart Home Watch

- **[Apple HomeView Launches Tomorrow, But Its First Security Update Is Already Here](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/12/apple-homeview-launches-tomorrow-but-its-first-security-update-is-already-here/)** — Apple patched nine vulnerabilities before most units even reached customers. If you do pick one up, update before setup. [SKIP]

- **[sonnen's New Smart Breakers Help Your Home Battery Last Longer](https://electrek.co/2026/11/12/sonnens-new-smart-breakers-help-your-home-battery-last-longer/)** — sonnen and SOLRITE are adding breakers that can shed non-essential loads automatically during a blackout, stretching how long a home battery covers you. Relevant if backup power is ever on your list for the house. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Lunar's New Home DC Fast Charger Puts Your EV Battery to Work](https://electrek.co/2026/11/12/lunars-new-home-dc-fast-charger-puts-your-ev-battery-to-work/)** — A 25kW home DC fast charger that, paired with Lunar's home battery system, also lets a compatible EV power the house during an outage and potentially sell power back to the grid. Same vehicle-to-home theme as the sonnen item above — this is becoming a real category, not a one-off. [READ DEEPER]
