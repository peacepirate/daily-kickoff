## TL;DR

Apple refreshed its smart home lineup yesterday — HomeView, HomePod mini 2, and a new Apple TV 4K — the most relevant launch of the week for an HA/Matter household. On the EV side, the US passed 80,000 public fast-charging ports and Q3 sales rebounded to a one-year high. Otherwise a quiet news day.

## What's New

- **[America Now Has More Than 80,000 Public Fast Charging Ports](https://insideevs.com/news/811678/us-ev-charging-growth-q3-2026/)** — Q3 added charging capacity faster and more broadly than prior quarters. So what: road-trip range anxiety keeps shrinking regardless of which EV you're tracking.
- **[EV Sales Rebound To Their Highest Level In A Year](https://insideevs.com/news/811825/ev-sales-q3-2026/)** — demand recovering after a rough stretch. So what: a useful data point if you're weighing whether the EV transition is stalling or just had a soft patch.
- **[Ford Opens Fathom EV Pickup Invite List Ahead of Launch](https://insideevs.com/news/811889/ford-fathom-ev-pickup-preorder-invite/)** — pre-orders open next year for Ford's sub-$30K EV pickup; invite list is open now. [SKIP]
- **[Apple Releases Third Public Betas of iOS 27.2 and macOS Golden Gate 27.2](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/13/apple-releases-ios-27-2-public-beta-3/)** — includes the redesigned Health app with new Insights and Longevity tabs. [SKIP]

## Smart Home Watch

- **[Welcome Home: Everything Apple Announced on October 13](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/13/apple-october-13-recap/)** — Apple shipped three smart home products without an event: the $299 HomeView (a new central hub with apps, widgets, and personalized content for up to six people), a refreshed $149 HomePod mini, and a new $299 Apple TV 4K with mics for Siri. So what: HomeView is a genuinely new category — a dedicated hub that competes with your existing Home Assistant setup rather than just another speaker. Worth watching for Matter/Thread interop before it earns a spot on the wall. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Apple's HomeView Repair Fees Revealed](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/13/apple-homeview-repair-fees/)** — $39 to repair with AppleCare+ ($2.49/mo or $24.99/yr), $279 without. So what: factor this into the buy decision if you're considering one for the kitchen or entryway.
- **[PSA: Wall-Mounted HomeView May Not Work If You Buy the Wrong Power Adapter](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/13/psa-wall-mounted-homeview-may-not-work-if-you-buy-the-wrong-power-adapter/)** — the wall-mount version doesn't ship with a power adapter, and the wrong one won't power it. So what: if you go wall-mount, check Apple's support doc for the compatible adapter spec before you buy — this is exactly the kind of gotcha that turns into a return.
