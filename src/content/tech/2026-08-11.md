## What's New
- **[Apple may introduce a photo authentication tool in iOS 27](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/10/ios-27-apple-reference-image/)** — A new "Reference" camera mode found in the iOS 27 beta would embed provenance data in photos so they can later be verified as iPhone-captured. Still unreleased, but a real answer to AI-fake proliferation rather than a rumor.

- **[The 2027 Kia EV3 Is Almost Here—With 280 Miles Of Range For The AWD Models](https://insideevs.com/news/804460/2027-kia-ev3-epa-range-official/)** — Official EPA numbers are in for Kia's affordable EV3, with AWD trims rated at 280 miles. A data point on how far sub-luxury EV range has come.

- **[Tesla Burned Through Its California EV Rebate Funds In Just Five Days](https://electrek.co/2026/08/10/tesla-california-myfirstev-rebate-exhausted-5-days/)** — California's new MyFirstEV rebate went live August 3 for Tesla buyers; the automaker's full allocation (~$18M) was gone by August 8. A sign of how much pent-up EV demand a modest rebate can unlock.

- **[GM Walks Away From $3.5B Battery Factory, Leaving Samsung SDI In Full Control](https://insideevs.com/news/804446/gm-sells-indiana-battery-factory-stake-samsung-sdi/)** — GM is exiting its Indiana battery joint venture, handing full control to Samsung SDI. Another data point on automakers pulling back from in-house battery bets.

- **[The Ford Fathom Will Get a $25,000 Hybrid Crossover Sibling: Report](https://insideevs.com/news/804399/ford-fathom-hybrid-crossover/)** — Dealers were shown an Escape-sized hybrid crossover expected to start around $25K. If it ships, it's a meaningful low end for the mainstream hybrid/EV crossover market.

- **[Anker's travel charger works in almost any country and is down to $20](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/977626/anker-nano-travel-adapter-summer-travel-tech-deal-sale)** — Anker's Nano Travel Adapter charges up to five devices at once and is small enough to actually pack. [BUY NOW]
