## TL;DR
Apple confirmed its September 9 event and a run of eight product launches this month — worth holding off on any Apple hardware purchase until the dust settles. Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.1 undercuts its own agentic-task pricing by up to 45%, worth a look for engineering tooling. Otherwise it's mostly IFA laptop/gaming noise, plus solid EV signal — a genuinely impressive Range Rover Electric first drive and a large-scale battery-aging study worth reading if you're EV shopping.

## What's New
- **[Anthropic launches Claude Fable 5.1 and says it's up to 45 percent cheaper for agentic work](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/987830/anthropic-claude-fable-mythos-5-1)** — Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 address customer complaints on price, data retention, and over-eager safeguards; Fable 5.1 runs ~25% cheaper than Fable 5 typically, up to 45% cheaper for complex agentic tasks. Worth checking if cost has been a factor for any team running Claude-based agents. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Apple Launching Eight New Products This Month](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/02/apple-launching-eight-new-products-next-month/)** — The September 9 "Surprise and shine" event kicks off a month with eight new products shipping: new iPhones, Watches, AirPods, plus the already-announced Mac mini and Mac Studio landing September 22. If you're due for an Apple hardware upgrade, wait two weeks. [WAIT]

- **[I Drove The $138,000 Range Rover Electric Off-Road. It Was Exceptional](https://insideevs.com/news/806401/range-rover-electric-specs-drive/)** — InsideEVs took Land Rover's first electric Range Rover off-road, five years after it was announced: 333 miles of real-world range with no compromise on comfort or capability. A useful benchmark for what "good" looks like at the luxury end of the EV transition. [READ DEEPER]

- **[This Study Covered 500,000 EV Batteries. Some Models Aged Much Better Than Others](https://insideevs.com/news/806798/ev-battery-aging-study-2026/)** — EV diagnostics company Aviloo checked battery health on 500,000 EVs across 20 popular models; one stood out for aging notably better than the rest. Worth more than sticker range if you're EV shopping. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Schneider's New Home EV Charger Undercuts Tesla's By Bundling A Big Feature](https://insideevs.com/news/806855/schneider-charge-home-ev-charger-load-balancing/)** — Schneider's new home charger includes load-balancing hardware in the box; Tesla's Wall Connector needs it as a separate add-on to use all your installed power. Worth knowing if a home charger install is ever on your list.

- **[CleanShot 5.0 with Studio Mode](https://www.producthunt.com/products/cleanshot)** — The Mac-native screenshot/recording tool shipped 5.0 with Studio Mode for collaborating on captures. If your teams already use CleanShot for docs or bug reports, worth a look. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Mac App Store Apps Can Now Drop Support for Intel Macs](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/01/mac-app-store-intel-mac-support/)** — Apple is letting developers of universal apps requiring macOS 13+ drop Intel support to simplify builds. Another small sign of the Intel Mac sunset accelerating; no action needed unless you're still running Intel hardware. [SKIP]
