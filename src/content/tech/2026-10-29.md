## TL;DR

Apple's new touchscreen MacBook Pro hands-on confirms it's a premium, first-gen product, not a spec bump — it still runs last year's M5 chip. Apple is finally fielding a real smart-home hub (HomeView) alongside a new HomePod mini, launching November 13. EV signal was steady but quiet: used EV SUVs are now selling for under $25k, and two mainstream efficiency updates (Kia EV9, Toyota hybrid) landed alongside a lab-tested BMW vs Mercedes EV SUV comparison.
