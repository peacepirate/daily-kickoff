## TL;DR
Apple has two bug fixes in flight: an iPhone 18 Pro reboot issue and a watchOS 27.0.1 patch for Apple Watch random restarts. Court filings show OpenAI privately calling ChatGPT-in-Siri "persistently underperforming" — a signal Apple's AI push is still struggling. On the EV side, US registrations fell 31% in July as hybrids surge, Rivian recalled ~99,000 R1/R2s over a camera fault, and Bentley's debut EV shows ultra-fast charging is now table stakes even in luxury SUVs.

## What's New
- **[Apple says software update to fix iPhone 18 Pro rebooting bug is coming next week](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/23/apple-iphone-18-pro-rebooting-bug-software-update-fix/)** — Confirms the freeze-and-reboot-on-failed-Face-ID issue reported this week is a software bug, not hardware. Fix lands early next week — if you're on an 18 Pro, no action needed beyond waiting for the update.
- **[Apple Releases watchOS 27.0.1 With Fix for Restart Bug](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/23/watchos-27-0-1-release/)** — Limited to Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4, fixes unexpected restarts. Update via the Watch app when it's on the charger.
- **[ChatGPT in Siri 'Persistently Underperforming,' Says OpenAI](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/23/openai-siri-chatgpt-underperforming/)** — Court filings in OpenAI's suit against xAI reveal the Siri integration disappointed OpenAI from launch — friction-heavy opt-in, weak adoption. Useful data point if you're forming a view on how fast (or slow) Apple's own AI stack is actually maturing. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Opera's free built-in VPN can now turn on automatically when using public Wi-Fi](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/24/operas-free-built-in-vpn-can-now-turn-on-automatically-when-using-public-wi-fi/)** — Auto-detects unsecured networks and enables the VPN without a manual toggle. Small, free default worth flagging to a team that travels. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
- **[EV Sales Plunge 31% In July, Hybrids Soar 86%](https://www.motor1.com/news/809401/us-ev-registrations-fall-31/)** — US EV demand softened sharply after federal credits expired, with Toyota, Ford and others leaning harder into hybrids. A real read on how much of prior EV growth was credit-driven rather than durable demand.
- **[The Rivian R2 Just Got Its First Recall](https://insideevs.com/news/809297/rivian-r1-r2-recall-camera/)** — ~99,000 R1 and R2 vehicles recalled for a camera-related safety issue. Worth knowing if Rivian is on your radar as a buy.
- **[Bentley unveils Torcal, its first EV: 888 PS, 375 miles of range](https://electrek.co/2026/09/23/bentley-torcal-first-electric-bentley-unveiled/)** — Not a purchase-relevant vehicle, but the fast-charging numbers (lunch-break-length top-ups on a 113kWh pack) are a marker of where charging tech across the industry is headed.
