## What's New
- **[Apple Plans iPhone-to-Windows Copy and Paste in EU After Microsoft Request](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/03/apple-iphone-windows-copy-paste/)** — Microsoft used Apple's EU interoperability request system (built for DMA compliance) to ask for cross-device copy-paste; Apple's been evaluating it since March and proposed a project plan in June. It's unclear yet if this ships outside the EU. Worth tracking if you regularly bridge iPhone and a Windows machine.
- **[Your Fitbit data can now connect directly to Apple Health](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/974778/google-health-fitbit-data-apple-health-syncing)** — Google Health's 5.05 update lets you sync steps, sleep, vitals, and workouts from Fitbit/Pixel Watch straight into Apple Health (HRV reportedly doesn't transfer yet). Useful if anyone in your household is on a mixed Fitbit/Apple setup and you want one consolidated health record.
- **[MOTHER](https://www.producthunt.com/products/mother-claude-code-terminal)** — A terminal built specifically around Claude Code, with one-click session resume. Worth a quick look if you or your ICs are running Claude Code day-to-day and want less friction picking sessions back up. `[SHARE w/ engineering team]`
- **[I found a 'secret menu' of ChatGPT features — here are my 9 favorites](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/chatgpt/i-found-a-secret-menu-of-chatgpt-features-here-are-my-9-favorites)** — Practical tips, including feeding ChatGPT/Gemini files straight from Google Drive instead of copy-pasting content into prompts. Low-effort productivity win worth passing along. `[SHARE w/ engineering team]`
- **[Audi's New A2 E-Tron Is Its Cheapest EV—And The Most Efficient Car It Has Ever Made](https://insideevs.com/news/803647/audi-a2-ev-efficiency-official/)** — With the optional efficiency package, the LFP-powered compact EV hit close to 5 miles/kWh in preliminary testing, launching this fall. A real efficiency milestone from a mainstream brand, not just a range-number stunt.
- **[BYD Claims Its New EV Can Go 683 Miles On A Charge—And Add Hundreds More In 5 Minutes](https://insideevs.com/news/803684/byd-denza-z9s-longest-range-ev-china/)** — China-only for now, but the range and charging-speed numbers (if they hold up outside lab conditions) are a preview of where battery tech is headed before it reaches mainstream US models.
