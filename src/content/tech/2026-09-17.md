## What's New

- **[Apple Watch Series 12 review: A new heart rate sensor and readiness score](https://www.tomsguide.com/wellness/smartwatches/apple-watch-series-12-review)** — Same design as prior generations, but the new Health Sensing System and a Whoop-style readiness score are the real story underneath. Multiple outlets (The Verge, Engadget, 9to5Mac) call it the biggest health-tracking jump in years for the Watch line, though the flagship audio features aren't live yet.

- **[watchOS 27 removes a useful Apple Watch feature](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/17/watchos-27-removes-useful-apple-watch-feature/)** — The new Live Rewind feature (double-click Digital Crown to replay the last 15 seconds of audio) uses the same gesture that switches between apps on watchOS 26. If you're on Series 12 or Ultra 4, that app-switching shortcut is effectively gone.

- **[Which iOS 27 CarPlay features actually work in your car?](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/17/which-ios-27-carplay-features-actually-work/)** — iOS 27's CarPlay update is out now, but the headline features (custom widgets, new dashboard layouts) only work on newer CarPlay hardware — most cars on the road today won't see much change.
