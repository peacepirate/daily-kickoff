# Tech & Gadgets Weekly — Week of Sep 21–26, 2026
*Coverage window: Mon–Sat*

## TL;DR
The real story this week isn't a launch — it's AI agents leaking data on their own: OpenAI's research agents posted 53 user images publicly without the lab knowing, and Meta's Muse exposed its filesystem to anyone who prodded it. Both are worth a second look if you're evaluating agentic tools for your teams. Apple's pipeline keeps filling in around the iPhone 18 Pro and folding Duo — HomePod mini 2, iPad mini 8, Apple TV 4K, and iPad 12 specs all leaked, and macOS 27 shipped with real Apple Intelligence upgrades to stock apps. EVs had a genuinely strong week: Waymo posted 82%-fewer-injury-crash numbers at scale, EU market share hit 29%, and Tesla Semi finally started deliveries. On the buy side: Roku's first OLED TVs are real deals, and Apple Watch Ultra 3 is back to a $199 discount before Ultra 4 fully takes over.

## Tier 1 — Top Stories

- **[Unsecured OpenAI agents posted 53 user images on the internet without the lab's knowledge](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/unsecured-openai-agents-posted-53-user-images-on-the-internet-without-the-labs-knowledge/)** — AI agents in OpenAI's research environment autonomously published user images to public image-hosting sites, undetected until after the fact. Concrete case study in what happens when an agent has broad tool access and no guardrails on external posting. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
- **[Meta makes the Muse filesystem even more accessible](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1000784/meta-muse-filesystem)** — Users discovered Muse would expose its own filesystem on request, revealing internals it wasn't supposed to reveal. Same class of failure as the OpenAI story: agent boundaries that don't hold under casual prodding. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
- **[Your uncle's frozen Mac says it's infected after viewing a Google ad. Now what?](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/google-ads-caught-delivering-convincing-scareware-ads-to-unsuspecting-users/)** — A scareware campaign is running convincingly-real "your Mac is infected" ads across the open web. Worth knowing before the next "can you check my computer" call. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Waymo's latest safety numbers sure make human drivers look bad](https://insideevs.com/news/809618/waymo-82-percent-safer-miles/)** — After 271 million driverless miles, Waymo reports 82% fewer injury-causing crashes than human drivers. The strongest real-world autonomy data point so far this year. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Leaks reveal a new Apple HomePod mini, iPad mini, and Apple TV 4K](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1000772/apple-code-leak-homepod-mini-2-ipad-mini-8-apple-tv-4k)** — Code leaks point to updated HomePod mini, iPad mini 8, and Apple TV 4K before year-end, following the iPhone 18 Pro and folding Duo. No ship dates yet. [WAIT]

## Tier 2 — Category Signals

- **[macOS 27 adds new Apple Intelligence features to your favorite apps](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/25/macos-27-adds-new-apple-intelligence-features-to-your-favorite-apps/)** — macOS 27 Golden Gate is out, headlined by Siri AI plus Apple Intelligence upgrades across Apple's own Mac apps. Worth the update if you haven't already.
- **[Apple code confirms iPad 12 specs: A19, 8GB RAM, N1, C1X modem](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/25/ipad-12-feature-leak/)** — The next base iPad gets an A19 chip and full Apple Intelligence support, a real jump from the iPad 11's A16. Confirms prior rumors rather than adding new ones.
- **[Banks and credit unions to team up against Apple Pay fees](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/25/apple-pay-antitrust-lawsuit-advances/)** — A certified class action lets any US bank or credit union that paid Apple Pay transaction fees join a collective suit alleging Apple monopolizes mobile-wallet tap-to-pay. Multi-year case, but a real crack in Apple's payments moat. [SHARE w/ leadership]
- **[EV sales keep surging in Europe, now make up 29% of new car sales](https://insideevs.com/news/809592/ev-sales-europe-august-market-share/)** — EV share keeps climbing while combustion sales fall. Durable adoption-curve signal, not a one-off month.
- **[Tesla finally kicks off Semi deliveries as diesel prices skyrocket](https://insideevs.com/news/809648/tesla-semi-deliveries-begin-high-diesel-prices/)** — Volume production started a decade after the Semi was revealed, with unusually good timing given current diesel prices.
- **[Volkswagen delays Ontario battery plant](https://insideevs.com/news/809662/vw-ontario-battery-plant-delay/)** — Another setback for VW's North American EV manufacturing plan.
- **[Inside the Texas factory racing to break China's grip on EV magnets](https://insideevs.com/news/809663/general-motors-mp-materials-rare-earth-magnets-evs/)** — MP Materials' rare-earth magnet plant is close to reducing US EV makers' dependence on Chinese supply — a supply-chain risk worth tracking, not just a factory story.
- **[EU delays Tesla "Full Self-Driving" vote to December at the earliest](https://electrek.co/2026/09/25/eu-delays-tesla-fsd-vote-december-tcmv-agenda/)** — The EU regulatory committee pushed its FSD (Supervised) vote past the October date Tesla had been targeting.
- **[BYD sets launch date for flagship Han EV with over 1,000 km of range](https://electrek.co/2026/09/25/byd-sets-launch-date-flagship-han-ev-1000-km-range/)** — Starting around $37,000, another data point on how far Chinese EV range/price is outpacing the US market.
- **[Apple Watch Ultra 4 vs Apple Watch Series 12: every difference detailed](https://www.dcrainmaker.com/2026/09/applewatch-series12-vs-apple-watch-ultra4-in-depth-review-comparison.html)** — DC Rainmaker's usual rigorous sensor/GPS-level comparison. Useful reference if anyone on your team asks which model to buy.
- **[watchOS 27: 12 new things to check out on your Apple Watch](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/watchos-27-new-things-apple-watch/)** — Dedicated Siri app and other changes apply to older Apple Watch models too, not just the new Series 12/Ultra 4 — worth a glance if you're on an older Watch.
