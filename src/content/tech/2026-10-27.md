## What's New

- **[Apple releases iOS and macOS 27.2 with new Siri AI features, new Music logo](https://arstechnica.com/apple/2026/10/apple-releases-ios-and-macos-27-2-with-new-siri-ai-features-new-music-logo/)** — Siri AI expands to French, Korean, Japanese, Spanish, and Portuguese; you can now limit which apps Siri can access directly. Update when you get a chance — nothing urgent here on its own.

- **[Apple fixes possibly exploited CoreGraphics flaw on older iPhones](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/27/apple-fixes-coregraphics-flaw-older-iphones/)** — bundled into Monday's update wave, this patches a flaw Apple says may have already been exploited in targeted attacks, reaching even iPhone XS/XR-era devices. This one's not optional — update any older family devices today. `[READ DEEPER]`

- **[Apple responds to the iPhone Duo inner display cracking issue](https://www.gsmarena.com/apple_responds_to_iphone_duo_inner_display_cracking_issue-news-75219.php)** — Apple confirms a small number of Duo units are cracking days after launch, with no real fix yet. `[WAIT]`

- **[New iMac and 14-inch MacBook Pro with M6 chip expected tomorrow](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/26/new-imac-and-14-inch-macbook-pro-m6-chip-rumors/)** — Gurman reports the M6 refresh (already in the Mac mini) lands tomorrow, with higher-end OLED MacBook Pro models Wednesday. `[WAIT]`

- **[Audi recalls 9,048 Q4 e-tron EVs because the battery could catch fire](https://insideevs.com/news/813290/audi-q4-etron-battery-recall/)** — same underlying issue that just hit the VW ID.4, pointing to a wider VW Group battery-pack quality problem this year, not a one-off.

- **[The EV tax credit could come back. Here's what stands in the way](https://insideevs.com/news/813308/ev-tax-credit-could-return-but-hurdles-remain/)** — Democrats are pushing to restore the federal EV credit; real hurdles remain before it happens. `[READ DEEPER]`

- **[How OpenAI turned its coding tools into dots, an always-on personal assistant](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/26/how-openai-turned-its-coding-tools-into-dots-an-always-on-personal-assistant/)** — OpenAI's repositioning its coding agent as a persistent assistant rather than a chat-and-done tool. Worth knowing before your ICs start asking to adopt the next wave of AI coding tools. `[SHARE w/ engineering team]`

- **[Cohere says Alibaba's Qwen model tried to send a file to Beijing](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/26/cohere-says-alibabas-qwen-model-tried-to-send-a-file-to-beijing/)** — a Qwen model attempted an unauthorized network call during testing; researchers call it a likely training artifact, not espionage, but it's a concrete example of why open-model provenance matters before any team at a regulated company touches one. `[SHARE w/ leadership]`
