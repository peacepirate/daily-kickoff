# Tech & Gadgets — Friday, November 20, 2026
*Coverage window: previous 24 hours*

## TL;DR
Apple's AI agent ambitions got real today — a job listing confirmed by Bloomberg and picked up independently by four outlets, the strongest signal yet of a Siri/agent overhaul. OpenAI pushed its Dots agents down to the $20/mo ChatGPT Plus tier. On EVs, GM's grid-payback pilot and Kia's sub-$25K target are both durable infrastructure/pricing signals, not hype.

## What's New

- **[Apple is hiring engineers to build its own AI agent](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1038227/apple-ai-agent-engineer-job-listing)** — A Bloomberg-sourced job listing describes an agent that browses the web, works across apps, writes and debugs code, and calls services — independently confirmed by Engadget, TechCrunch, and MacRumors today, which is unusual corroboration for a job-posting leak. This is Apple's answer to Meta Muse and OpenAI Dots, and it's the first concrete sign Siri's overhaul has real engineering weight behind it. [READ DEEPER]

- **[OpenAI is testing Dots personal agents on its $20/month Plus tier](https://www.engadget.com/2322656/openai-is-testing-dots-personal-agents-on-its-20-per-month-plus-tier/)** — Dots — proactive agents that work unsupervised — was Pro/Enterprise-only until now. Pushing it to the mass-market Plus tier is the move that actually puts it in front of a team, not just power users. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Second iOS 27.3 and macOS 27.3 public betas are out](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/19/macos-27-3-public-beta-2-now-available-heres-everything-new/)** — Safari gets a Vertical Tabs layout and Adaptive Dark Mode; Siri can now act across more of Apple's own apps, with developer hooks for third-party Siri actions coming. Routine beta cycle, nothing urgent to act on yet.

- **[AirPods Pro 3 drop to $179, AirPods Max 2 to $429 for Black Friday](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/19/airpods-black-friday-deals/)** — The Pro 3 price matches October's Prime Day low; Max 2 is the first meaningful discount since launch. If you've been waiting on either, this is the window before Black Friday proper. [BUY NOW]

- **[GM's V2G pilot wants to pay California drivers for power from parked EVs](https://electrek.co/2026/11/19/gms-v2g-pilot-wants-to-pay-california-drivers-for-power-from-parked-evs/)** — GM Energy and WeaveGrid will cover equipment/install costs (up to $16K) and pay eligible SCE customers for grid power sent back from their EV batteries. Still California-only and utility-specific, but it's a real economic model for vehicle-to-grid, not a concept demo. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Kia's cheapest EV is coming next year, and it could cost under $25,000](https://electrek.co/2026/11/19/kias-cheapest-ev-coming-2027-under-25k/)** — Kia is targeting roughly $22K–24K for a 2027 model, filling the one price tier its EV lineup still lacks. A real signal that sub-$25K EVs are becoming a 2027 category, not a one-off. [WAIT]
