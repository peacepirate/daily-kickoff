## What's New
- **[Chrome adopts what may be the best protection yet against account takeovers](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/08/chrome-adopts-what-may-be-the-best-protection-yet-against-account-takeovers/)** — Chrome now binds session cookies to the device, so a stolen cookie can't be replayed on another machine. Closes off one of the most common account-hijacking techniques — worth flagging given your regulated-industry threat model. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Grok Bot brings always-on AI agents to macOS and iOS](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/11/grok-bot-macos-ios/)** — xAI's new agents run in the cloud (they keep working with your laptop closed) and can sign into sites and complete tasks from a single chat message, tied to the Cursor acquisition. Early, but worth a look if you're evaluating agentic AI tooling for your teams. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[I'm already obsessed with iOS 27's one-tap suggestions in Messages](https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/im-already-obsessed-with-ios-27s-one-tap-suggestions-in-messages-heres-why)** — iOS 27 beta adds one-tap suggested replies and actions in Messages, cutting typing for routine responses. Small but genuinely useful when it ships.

- **[Kia Just Revealed EV3 Pricing. It's Even Cheaper Than We Expected](https://insideevs.com/news/804556/2027-kia-ev3-prices-revealed/)** — The 2027 EV3 starts at $29,890, undercutting expectations and landing right alongside the Nissan Leaf and Chevy Bolt with more range. Good data point on where the affordable-EV floor is settling.

- **[Used EV buying guide: The $30,000 edition](https://arstechnica.com/cars/2026/08/used-ev-buying-guide-the-30000-edition/)** — A practical walkthrough of what's actually worth buying used at that price point, model by model. [READ DEEPER]

- **[EVgo And GM's Gas Station-Like Flagship Charging Network Is Growing Fast](https://insideevs.com/news/804596/evgo-gm-flagship-ev-charging-stations-update/)** — The latest station opened near Detroit with 350kW pull-through stalls and a canopy — laid out like a gas station rather than the usual awkward EV stall row. Sign that fast-charging infrastructure is maturing toward road-trip-usable.

- **[Sonos Preparing AirPods Max Rival With Voice Assistant Support](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/11/sonos-ace-ultra/)** — An FCC filing points to a "Sonos Ace Ultra" headphone with AI voice assistant support, likely debuting at Sonos's September event as an AirPods Max/Sony XM6 rival. Hold off if you're headphone-shopping. [WAIT]
