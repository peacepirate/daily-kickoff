## What's New

- **[Apple explains how the iPhone 18 Pro's Reference Image mode works](https://security.apple.com/blog/apple-reference-image/)** — Apple's security team detailed the cryptographic architecture behind Reference Image, which proves a photo was captured by a real iPhone sensor and hasn't been AI-edited, likening it to a digital film negative. Worth understanding now — this is the kind of provenance tech that'll matter for verifying content at work as AI-generated fakes get harder to spot. [READ DEEPER]

- **OmniFocus adds Siri AI support** as the Omni Group updates its app suite for iOS 27/macOS 27. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/15/omnifocus-adds-siri-ai-support-as-omni-group-updates-its-apps-for-ios-27-macos-27-more/))

- **[The 2027 Ford F-150 unlocks hands-free towing through BlueCruise](https://insideevs.com/news/808408/ford-bluecruise-f150-handsfree-towing/)** — Ford's driver-assist system now works with a trailer attached, with some limitations. Notable as hands-free driving moves from commuting into towing, a much harder control problem.

- **[Kia's PV7 electric van goes 800V, with flexible body styles](https://insideevs.com/news/808421/kia-pv7-800v-charging/)** — The bigger sibling to Kia's PV5 gets 800V fast-charging architecture. Minivan-shaped EVs are quietly becoming a real category again — sales were up 21% in 2025.

- **[NHTSA orders Tesla to prove its Cybercab is legal to sell, under oath](https://electrek.co/2026/09/15/nhtsa-tesla-cybercab-special-order-fmvss-certification/)** — The agency issued a formal Special Order demanding Tesla show how a vehicle with no steering wheel, pedals, or mirrors meets federal safety standards written for human drivers. Tesla has until September 30 to respond, or face penalties up to $139 million. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Demand for EV chargers is outstripping supply, ChargePoint reports](https://arstechnica.com/cars/2026/09/ev-charging-infrastructure-is-still-lagging-adoption-report-says/)** — EV adoption is growing faster than charging infrastructure can keep up, even in the US. A useful data point if you're weighing home charging investment against relying on public infrastructure.
