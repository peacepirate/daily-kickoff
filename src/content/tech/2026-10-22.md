# Tech & Gadgets — Thursday, October 22, 2026
*Coverage window: previous 24 hours*

## TL;DR
A PHP vulnerability knocked major sites offline today — worth a heads-up to your team even though it's not your stack. Tesla's Q3 earnings landed: Cybercab goes on sale to customers by year-end, but unsupervised FSD for owners slips again, now to Q2 2027 and robotaxi-zones-only. Apple was ordered to open the iPhone to rival app stores in Australia. A software-budget survey and a new Anthropic agent launch are the two items most worth your manager hat today.

## What's New

- **[PHP: The three letters that shut down sites all over the internet today](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1015906/php2shell-vulnerability-october-2026)** — Two critical PHP vulnerabilities took down 4chan, Wikipedia, DeviantArt, and a wide swath of WordPress sites today. Not your stack, but worth a quick heads-up to engineering in case anything downstream touches PHP. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Tesla pushes unsupervised FSD to Q2 2027, and only inside Robotaxi zones](https://electrek.co/2026/10/21/tesla-unsupervised-fsd-q2-2027-robotaxi-fleet/)** — Tesla's Q3 call confirmed Cybercab goes on sale to customers by end of 2026, but "unsupervised" FSD for owner-owned cars is delayed again and will initially only work inside Tesla's own robotaxi zones, not wherever you want to drive. If you're weighing the $99/mo FSD subscription, this is another reason to hold off. [WAIT]

- **[Seattle Is Installing Hundreds Of Curbside EV Chargers](https://insideevs.com/news/812869/seattle-curbside-ev-chargers-2030/)** — Residents get to pick the Level 2 charger locations, prioritizing apartment buildings without home charging. A useful model if Richmond ever expands curbside charging — the resident-driven siting is the part worth copying.

- **[It's finally here: Porsche puts wireless charging into production](https://electrek.co/2026/10/21/its-finally-here-porsche-puts-wireless-ev-charging-into-production-video/)** — Porsche's Cayenne Electric SUV now offers inductive wireless charging as a factory option. First mainstream-adjacent production car to ship it — a signal the tech is past the demo stage.

- **[Enterprises keep buying software upgrades that IT thinks will make things worse](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2026/10/survey-enterprises-waste-28-percent-of-software-budgets-on-unwanted-upgrades/)** — Survey of 2,018 IT heads at large firms: 28% of software budgets go to upgrades IT didn't want, mostly because of vendor lock-in and lack of leverage. Good data point if you're building a case against a forced upgrade cycle. [SHARE w/ leadership]

- **[Apple Ordered to Open iPhone to Rival App Stores in Australia](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/22/rival-app-stores-in-australia-ordered/)** — Australia's Federal Court ordered Apple to allow competing app stores, sideloading, and alternative payments starting March 2027, following the 2025 antitrust ruling. First real crack in App Store exclusivity outside the EU. [READ DEEPER]
