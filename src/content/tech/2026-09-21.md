## What's New

- **[Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 Owners Report Unexpected Reboots](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/21/apple-watch-series-12-ultra-4-reported-reboots/)** — New Ultra 4 and Series 12 units are randomly rebooting to the Apple logo during normal use (Siri AI, Maps, workouts). Diagnostic logs point to the Neural Engine. Nothing on a fix timeline yet. [WAIT]

- **[Siri AI Settlement Website Now Live: Apple to Pay Some iPhone Owners](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/20/siri-ai-settlement-website-now-live/)** — Apple's $250M settlement over Siri's delayed launch is now accepting claims (Sept 21–Dec 21), roughly $25–95 per eligible iPhone. Worth five minutes if you owned an affected iPhone.

- **[iFixit Shares iPhone 18 Pro Teardown With Inside Look at New Features](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/20/ifixit-iphone-18-pro-teardown/)** — Confirms the new variable-aperture camera hardware and slightly larger batteries (18 Pro Max up 9.4% over last year). iFixit also flagged a display repair concern — read before deciding on AppleCare. [READ DEEPER]

- **[This VW ID. Buzz Finished A 62,000-Mile World Trip With Nearly 96% Battery Health](https://insideevs.com/news/808905/vw-id-buzz-world-trip/)** — 92 countries, 445 days, zero technical breakdowns, and only ~4% battery degradation. Rare real-world longevity data point for EV batteries rather than a lab estimate.

- **[From 10-97% SOC in 9 minutes: Sunwoda joins the flash charging race](https://electrek.co/2026/09/20/from-10-97-soc-in-9-minutes-sunwoda-joins-the-flash-charging-race/)** — Chinese battery maker claims ~62 miles of range added per minute of charging. Still lab conditions, not deployed infrastructure, but the fast-charging trendline keeps moving.

- **[Review: Satechi's Thunderbolt 5 CubeDock pairs perfectly with the new M6 Mac mini](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/20/satechi-thunderbolt-5-cubedock-mac-mini-review/)** — A dedicated TB5 dock built around the M6 Mac mini's form factor. Relevant if you're upgrading from an M4-era mini. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Vocci's ring adds a new form factor to meeting note-taking](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/20/voccis-ring-adds-a-new-form-factor-to-meeting-note-taking/)** — A $249 ring that records and transcribes meetings without a phone on the table. Interesting for note-taking workflows, but it's a wearable always-on mic — worth the privacy conversation before rolling out to a team. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
