## TL;DR

EV news dominates today — Genesis unveiled its GV90 luxury electric SUV, Nevada opened the door to large-scale commercial robotaxis for Tesla/Uber/Waymo, and a Geely solid-state battery test hints at 600+ mile range EVs by 2027. On the Apple side, ChatGPT can now read and send iMessages on Mac, and early code details surfaced for camera-equipped AirPods. No Home Assistant, Matter, or Zigbee news today.

## What's New

- **[ChatGPT can now read and send iMessages on Mac](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/20/chatgpt-imessages-mac/)** — OpenAI shipped a Messages plugin for ChatGPT that can search, draft, and send iMessages/SMS from prompts like "reply to Dave with times I'm free next week." A concrete example of agentic assistant UX with real account access — the kind of pattern your teams may end up building internally. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Google's Antigravity coding agent ships as editor extensions](https://www.producthunt.com/products/google-antigravity)** — Instead of a standalone IDE, Antigravity's agents now live inside your existing editor. Lowers the switching cost for teams evaluating in-editor AI coding agents. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Nevada approves paid robotaxi service for Uber, Tesla, and Waymo](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/20/tesla-uber-and-waymo-all-get-the-ok-to-operate-thousands-of-robotaxis-in-nevada/)** — Permits allow up to 8,000 robotaxis in the state over the next 12 months. Regulatory friction on commercial autonomy is easing faster than expected. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Genesis reveals the GV90, a three-row electric SUV with coach doors and heated floors](https://www.theverge.com/transportation/982910/genesis-gv90-ev-suv-coach-door-heated-floor)** — Hyundai's luxury brand's first full-size US electric SUV, with rear-hinged coach doors, a retractable screen, and an optional 4-seat VIP cabin. Not a buy signal for you, but a marker of how far EV design has moved upmarket.

- **[Hyundai and Genesis EVs get a bigger discount at Ionna chargers](https://insideevs.com/news/805688/hyundai-genesis-ev-ionna-charging-discount/)** — Two stackable discounts make Ionna some of the cheapest DC fast charging in the country for Hyundai/Genesis owners. Useful reference point if EV charging cost comes up.

- **[Geely tests a solid-state battery with 621+ miles of range — could land in a Volvo](https://insideevs.com/news/805618/volvo-geely-solid-state-2027/)** — Targeting 2027. If it ships, it resets the range conversation for the whole EV segment. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Camera AirPods: code reveals resolution, status light, person detection](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/21/camera-airpods-code-reveals-hardware-details/)** — Found in the macOS Tahoe 26.7 release candidate: a new "AccessorySensorManager" framework describes color image capture from both earbuds. Early, but concrete — worth knowing before your next AirPods purchase.

- **[Tesla discontinues its Solar Roof tiles](https://electrek.co/2026/08/20/tesla-discontinues-solar-roof-panels-only/)** — Tesla told installers it's dropping the tile product entirely, keeping only standard solar panels. If solar's ever on your home-automation roadmap, tiles are off the table.
