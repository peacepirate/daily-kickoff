## TL;DR
VW confirmed its ID.4 replacement will be called the ID. Tiguan, and BYD's cheap Seagull EV now claims 5-minute charging — both signals the EV pace keeps accelerating. On the Apple side: iOS 27 smooths Apple Pay card switching, the 16-inch MacBook Pro M5 Max just got a real discount, and a new Vision Pro is reportedly in the works with lighter form factors. The Verge's smart-home graveyard piece is worth a skim before buying any more connected appliances.

Keeps current ID.4 clearance pricing in play as a closeout, not a permanent deal.

- **[Level 1, 2 and 3 EV chargers: Which is better to use?](https://www.engadget.com/2267893/level-1-2-vs-3-ev-charger-which-is-better/)** — Plain explainer: Level 1 (standard outlet) covers most daily driving if you're not doing long trips regularly. [READ DEEPER]
- **[iOS 27 makes a great change to Apple Pay on iPhone](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/27/ios-27-makes-it-easier-to-switch-between-apple-pay-cards/)** — Redesigned checkout flow makes it easier to switch cards and see order details mid-purchase, in apps and on the web.
- **[Amazon Just Knocked $900 Off Apple's Latest 16-Inch MacBook Pro](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/27/2026-macbook-pro-900-off/)** — M5 Max, 32-core GPU, 36GB RAM, 2TB SSD config is $3,499, down from $4,399. [BUY NOW]
- **[Report: Apple working on new Vision Pro, with different form factors on the table](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/27/apple-working-on-new-lighter-vision-pro-version/)** — Bloomberg reports Apple shelved the lighter "Vision Air" project last year but is now working on a new lighter-headset effort. Early-stage, no timeline.
