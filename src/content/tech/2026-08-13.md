# Tech & Gadgets — Thursday, August 13, 2026
*Coverage window: previous 24 hours*

## TL;DR
Google's Pixel 11 launch dominated the day, but it's Android-side noise for you — Apple's base iPhone 18 slipping to Q1 2027 is the more relevant Apple signal. On the EV side, a real-world 78,000-mile Tesla LFP battery test and rising EV transaction prices are worth a look if you're tracking the used or new market. Claude Cowork landing in a Chrome sidebar is the one genuinely useful productivity update today.

## What's New
- **[It looks like Apple's iPhone 18 really will skip the fall launch this year](https://www.theverge.com/tech/979231/apple-base-iphone-18-launch-delayed)** — Apple supplier Pegatron confirmed on an earnings call that the base iPhone 18 won't ship until Q1 2027; only the Pro models and a new iPhone Air arrive this fall. If you were timing an upgrade around a base-model refresh, push that expectation out. [WAIT]

- **[Claude Cowork can now run in a Chrome sidebar](https://www.engadget.com/2235919/claude-cowork-can-now-run-in-a-chrome-sidebar/)** — Anthropic's browser extension puts Claude Cowork alongside whatever page you're on instead of a separate window. Small friction reduction, but worth a look for anyone doing research or drafting in-browser. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[They Bought A 78,000-Mile Tesla With An Unknown Past. Then They Tested The Battery](https://insideevs.com/news/804780/tesla-model-3-lfp-degradation/)** — An independent battery test on a high-mileage Model 3 with unknown charging history came back healthy but unremarkable. Good real-world data point on LFP battery longevity if the used-EV market is on your radar. [READ DEEPER]

- **[US EV prices are going back up as discounts shrink](https://electrek.co/2026/08/12/us-ev-prices-are-going-back-up-as-discounts-shrink/)** — After six straight months of year-over-year declines, average EV transaction prices are rising again as incentives fade. If you've been waiting for a discount window on a new EV, that window is closing.

- **[Study: DC Fast Charging Satisfaction Is Up, And Ionna Takes The Top Spot](https://insideevs.com/news/804743/ev-charging-satifsfaction-2026-jdpower/)** — JD Power's latest charging-experience survey shows overall satisfaction improving, with the Ionna network (the GM/Honda/Hyundai-backed joint venture) now rated highest. Infrastructure signal, not an action item.

- **[Google Pixel Watch 5 Hands-On: Most Accurate GPS Watch Ever?](https://www.dcrainmaker.com/2026/08/google-pixel-watch-5-gps-accuracy-vs-garmin-apple.html)** — DC Rainmaker's lab test found the Pixel Watch 5's new GPS algorithms notably more accurate than prior Pixel and comparable Apple Watch models. Not your platform, but a useful data point on where wearable GPS accuracy is heading.
