## What's New

- **[Google's Gemini app is now on Apple CarPlay](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/11/google-gemini-apple-carplay/)** — Gemini conversations sync from your iPhone to CarPlay, so you can pick up a conversation (e.g. "give me my daily brief") from the dashboard. Works alongside Siri — no need to abandon your assistant setup.

- **[United won't let AI agents book flights](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1031623/united-unauthorized-ai-agents)** — United is blocking personal AI assistants from booking tickets, citing privacy and regulatory concerns; DoorDash and others have made similar calls. A concrete early signal of how businesses are drawing lines around agent access. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Apple is shuttering its 28-year-old support forum](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/11/apple-is-shuttering-its-28-year-old-online-community-forum/)** — The Apple Support Community goes read-only now, fully closes November 20. No replacement announced yet; if you've ever searched an old thread for a fix, archive what you need before then.

- **[A BMW i3 drove 3,658 miles on under 3 hours of total charging](https://insideevs.com/news/815161/bmw-i3-norway-to-spain/)** — Norway to southern Spain, 2 hours 46 minutes of charging total. A useful real-world data point on how far charging-network maturity has come, even on an older EV.

- **[Europe's EV sales jumped 49% in October](https://electrek.co/2026/11/11/europes-electric-car-sales-jump-49-as-global-ev-sales-grow/)** — Global EV sales grew 8%, with North America posting its first year-over-year increase in 2026 — though Electrek flags a catch on the North American number worth reading if you're tracking EV adoption trends.

- **[Three AI coding-agent tools launched on Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/crossfade)**: [Crossfade](https://www.producthunt.com/products/crossfade) lets a coding agent design inside an open Figma file, [Foxpit](https://www.producthunt.com/products/foxpit) runs Claude Code/Codex/OpenCode as one team, and [Sluice](https://www.producthunt.com/products/sluice-2) gives you room to run ~20 agents at once on a Mac. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Lenovo Yoga 9n 2-in-1 vs. MacBook Pro, two-week test](https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/laptops/lenovo-yoga-9n-2-in-1-review)** — Great OLED screen and local AI features, but gaming and raw performance trail the MacBook Pro, at a higher price. No case to switch. [WAIT]
