## What's New

- **[Apple's Siri AI Can Be Swapped Out for Claude, ChatGPT, Code Shows](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/14/siri-can-be-swapped-out-for-chatgpt-claude/)** — Code found in iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate shows a "Model Delegation" mechanism: Claude can appear as a Siri extension the same way ChatGPT already does, letting a user pick which model actually handles a request from the system-wide "Ask..." menu. Not shipped yet, but it's a real architectural hook, not a rumor. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Apple is reportedly working on iPhone game controllers](https://www.theverge.com/tech/994426/apple-iphone-game-controllers)** — Bloomberg's Gurman says Apple is designing two controllers, likely under the Beats brand, corroborating references found in macOS beta code. Not something you'd buy, but a sign Apple's investing more in iPhone as a gaming device.

- **[iOS 27: 7 features you need to try first after updating](https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/ios-27-7-features-you-need-to-try-first-after-updating)** — A rundown of the upgrades worth finding on your own phone now that iOS 27 is out, skipping the menu-hunting.

- **[Apple planning three major changes for its retail stores, per report](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/13/apple-planning-three-major-changes-for-its-retail-stores-per-report/)** — Bloomberg reports Apple is bringing back dedicated Genius Bars, among other retail changes. Relevant if you ever need in-person Apple support.

- **[Volkswagen Just Unveiled An EV That Can Do Over 9 Miles/kWh](https://insideevs.com/news/808168/volkswagen-mission-efficiency-concept-range-charging-test/)** — VW's "near-production" Mission Efficiency concept shares underpinnings with the ID. Polo but is tuned purely for efficiency — a useful marker for where mainstream EV efficiency is headed next.

- **[Ionna's EV Charging Network Grew 50% In Just Six Months](https://insideevs.com/news/808127/ionna-growth-50-percent-2026/)** — The manufacturer-backed charging network (built by GM, Honda, Hyundai, and others) keeps expanding fast — good signal for anyone weighing charging-network coverage as part of an EV purchase decision.

- **[After Gemini, another Google app may be coming to macOS](https://www.androidauthority.com/google-apple-mac-app-apk-teardown-3710852/)** — Teardown evidence suggests a second Google app is headed to Mac, following Gemini. Early and unconfirmed, but worth a glance if you use Google's apps alongside Apple hardware.
