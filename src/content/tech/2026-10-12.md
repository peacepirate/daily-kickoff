## TL;DR

Apple's smart-home event lands tomorrow (Oct 13) with a new hub called HomeView, HomePod mini 2, and Apple TV 4K all but confirmed via leaks — hold off on any hub-adjacent purchase until then. A new ambient-recording AI app from a Plaud alum is launching for iPhone/Mac/Watch, worth a careful look given the always-listening trade-off. Tesla's Robotaxi fleet is now under a third federal safety review in five weeks, this one over school-zone speeding.

## What's New

- **[New Apple TV 4K, HomeView, and HomePod Mini: 16 Details Just Leaked](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/11/apple-tv-4k-homeview-homepod-mini-2-leaks/)** — Bloomberg's Gurman says Apple's touch-display smart home hub will be named HomeView, run an A18 chip with 8GB RAM, support Siri AI, and ship in tabletop and wall-mounted forms alongside a new HomePod mini and Apple TV 4K, all unveiled Tuesday and on shelves Oct 30. This is the hub to evaluate against your existing Home Assistant/Matter setup before buying anything else in the category. [WAIT]

- **[OLED iPad Mini Could Be $200 More Expensive Than Current Model](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/12/oled-ipad-mini-more-expensive-than-current-model/)** — Gurman expects the OLED iPad mini refresh to land around $799 versus the current $599, alongside a new A20 Pro chip and redesigned speakers. If $599-class specs already cover your use case, buy the current model before it's gone. [BUY NOW]

- **[Former Plaud exec raises $26M for AI app that listens to you in the background](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/12/former-plaud-exec-raises-26m-for-ai-app-that-listens-to-you-in-the-background/)** — Ambi is shipping ambient-capture apps for iPhone, Mac, and Apple Watch that record and summarize conversations continuously rather than on-demand. Before anyone on your teams tries an always-listening note-taker, it's worth checking what that means for client calls and anything covered by your compliance obligations. [SHARE w/ leadership]

- **[NHTSA weighs probe into claims Tesla Robotaxis speed in school zones](https://electrek.co/2026/10/12/nhtsa-tesla-robotaxi-school-zone-speeding-petition/)** — A defect petition covering roughly 1,000 Model Y and Cybercab robotaxis is the third federal action against Tesla's driverless fleet in five weeks. A concrete data point on how far ahead of oversight an autonomous system can run before regulators catch up — the same governance question that applies to agentic AI tools. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Inside the mini factory where GM builds its EV future](https://www.theverge.com/transportation/1008023/gm-bcdc-ev-battery-factory-china-tesla)** — GM's Warren, Michigan campus is building out next-generation battery cell production, a quiet but concrete signal on how committed the company is to EVs versus near-term hybrids.

- **[macOS 27 brings three key upgrades to my favorite feature](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/11/macos-27-brings-three-key-upgrades-to-iphone-mirroring/)** — iPhone Mirroring got three real improvements in this fall's macOS/iOS release. If you already use it daily to work your iPhone from your Mac, worth checking what changed. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
