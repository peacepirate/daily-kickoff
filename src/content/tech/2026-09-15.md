## What's New

- **[Apple releases iOS 27, macOS Golden Gate 27 with Siri AI and Liquid Glass refinements](https://arstechnica.com/apple/2026/09/apple-releases-ios-27-macos-golden-gate-27-with-siri-ai-and-liquid-glass-refinements/)** — Apple shipped iOS 27, macOS Golden Gate 27, iPadOS 27, watchOS 27, and visionOS 27 today. Siri AI is English-only beta for now (EU excluded until later), and this is the last macOS version to support Rosetta for Intel apps — plan your Mac migration accordingly if you're still running Intel-only software. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Microsoft issues emergency Windows 11 update to fix its record-breaking patch](https://www.theverge.com/news/995302/microsoft-out-of-band-windows-11-update-fix-issues)** — This month's Patch Tuesday fixed nearly 1,000 flaws, Microsoft's biggest update ever, but broke Hyper-V folder shares and other business systems. An out-of-band fix is rolling out now — worth flagging to whoever owns Windows patching before the cumulative update goes wide. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[2028 Volvo XC60 And XC90 Plug-In Hybrids Get A Massive EV Range Boost](https://insideevs.com/news/807862/2028-volvo-xc60-xc90-long-range-plug-in-hybrid/)** — The refreshed PHEVs more than double their EV-only range, with new Gemini-powered infotainment on top. A concrete signal that plug-in hybrid range is closing in on "good enough for daily driving without gas," even outside Tesla/BYD-style EVs.

- **[The Chevy Silverado EV is now America's most affordable full-size electric pickup](https://electrek.co/2026/09/14/chevy-silverado-ev-most-affordable-full-size-ev-pickup/)** — With the Ford F-150 Lightning discontinued, Silverado EV takes the affordability crown in full-size electric trucks.

- **[Toyota is delaying the Highlander EV, but there's a bigger problem brewing](https://electrek.co/2026/09/14/toyota-delaying-highlander-ev-creates-bigger-problem/)** — The electric Highlander's launch is slipping just as the gas version phases out of production — a supply gap of Toyota's own making, not a demand problem.

## Smart Home Watch

- **[Apple Home's new security camera features cost as much as $60 a month](https://www.theverge.com/tech/994949/apple-intelligence-apple-home-icloud-plus-cost-subscription)** — iOS 27 brings Apple Intelligence video summaries to HomeKit Secure Video — AI-generated text descriptions of what your cameras saw — but it's gated behind pricier iCloud+ tiers. If you're running HomeKit cameras, check which tier actually includes it before assuming your current plan covers it. [READ DEEPER]
