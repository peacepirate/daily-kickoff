If you're due for a MacBook Air, it just hit an all-time-low price. Two small agent-orchestration tools surfaced on Product Hunt worth a look for your engineering teams.

- **[Apple Launching Five New Products This Friday](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/02/apple-launching-five-new-products-this-friday/)** — iPad mini (OLED, A20 Pro), iMac M6, and 14"/16" MacBook Pro with M6 — including the first OLED touchscreen MacBook Pro — all ship November 6. If a Mac refresh is on your list, pricing starts at $1,999 (M6 MacBook Pro) and $2,899 (14" OLED touch model).

## Smart Home Watch

- **[Apple's HomeView Could Double as a Security Camera](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/02/apple-homeview-could-work-security-camera/)** — Code strings in HomeView software 27.2 point to a HomeKit Secure Video mode (1080p/30fps, motion detection, home/away/asleep scheduling). Worth tracking if you'd fold it into your existing HA/Matter camera setup instead of buying a dedicated one. Related: **[HomeView ships with six-month-old software out of the box](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/01/apple-homeview-six-month-old-software/)** — you'll need to update immediately on setup (device launches Nov. 13).

- **[HomePod Mini 2 First Look](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1024108/homepod-mini-2-hands-on-siri-smart-home-mac-speaker)** — $149, six years after the original. Chip bump, better AirPlay, no major smart-home capability jump. Skippable unless your current Mini has died.
