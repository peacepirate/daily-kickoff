Apple's smart home push keeps building momentum — Schneider's new thermostat uses your iPhone's location to time HVAC around cheaper or cleaner power, and hands-on time with HomeView suggests Apple nailed the boring-but-useful part of a home dashboard. A fridge-hacking incident at 700 supermarkets is a reminder that unpatched IoT gear is still the weak link worth checking on your own network.
