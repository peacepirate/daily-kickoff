## TL;DR

Apple was the week's main story on two fronts: a genuine leadership change (John Ternus replacing Tim Cook as CEO) and a countdown to the September 9 event, where the first foldable iPhone, new Watches, and AirPods are expected. Home Assistant shipped a real quality-of-life release for anyone with Modbus-based energy hardware. EV news skewed toward friction — a federal investigation into Tesla's steering-wheel-less Cybercab, and a charging-infrastructure story showing how bad "public" dealer chargers can still be. Beyond that, it was a quiet week: Android/Samsung/Xiaomi foldable chatter was mostly pre-positioning ahead of Apple's event, and nothing else cleared the bar for durable signal.

## Tier 1 — Top Stories

- **[Top Stories: 'Surprise and Shine' Apple Event Preview, John Ternus Takes Over, and More](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/05/top-stories-suprise-and-shine-preview/)** — Tim Cook has handed the CEO role to John Ternus, previously Apple's hardware engineering chief, and Apple's September 9 event is confirmed with a foldable iPhone expected for the first time. Two structural changes in one week — worth tracking for how it shapes Apple's product cadence. [SHARE w/ leadership]
- **[iPhone Ultra, iPhone 18 Pro, and More: Apple's September 9 Event Preview](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/apple-september-2026-what-to-expect/)** — Confirmed for Wednesday, Sept 9, 10am PT: a foldable iPhone, new iPhone 18 lineup, new Apple Watches, and new AirPods. If you're due for an upgrade on any of these, the smart move is to wait four days. [WAIT]
- **[Apple Planning AI Home Security Camera and Service for 2027](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/04/apple-home-security-camera-2027/)** — Bloomberg reports Apple is building a privacy-forward home security camera that analyzes the environment with on-device AI rather than storing video, paired with a new home hub. Directly relevant if you're building out Matter/Thread camera coverage and want to avoid committing to a competing ecosystem right before Apple enters it. [READ DEEPER]
- **[2026.9: There's room on this bus](https://www.home-assistant.io/blog/2026/09/02/release-20269/)** — Home Assistant's September release adds a proper Modbus integration layer: devices that speak Modbus (solar inverters, heat pumps, energy meters) can now share one connection and get picked in the UI instead of requiring a hand-written register map in YAML. A real reduction in setup friction for a previously walled-off device category. [READ DEEPER]
- **[Tesla's Cybercab has been deployed, and it's already under investigation](https://arstechnica.com/cars/2026/09/teslas-cybercab-has-been-deployed-and-its-already-under-investigation/)** — The steering-wheel-less robotaxi launched in Austin and the US government opened a safety-standards investigation within hours. Signal on how fast regulators are moving on true no-driver-controls vehicles.
