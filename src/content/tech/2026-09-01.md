## TL;DR
Apple leadership just changed the guard — Tim Cook is out as CEO, John Ternus is in, and Phil Schiller stepped back from the App Store and Apple events the same day. Otherwise it's a quiet day for gear you'd actually buy — a Qualcomm chip price hike and a camera-equipped AirPods rumor are the only near-term Apple hardware signals, and EV news is incremental (Honda/Nissan ECU alliance, VW ID.4 successor spy shots). The one story worth pausing on is a second fatal Tesla Autopilot freeway incident — worth a closer look if you track EV safety data.

## What's New
- **[Tim Cook Steps Down as CEO of Apple, John Ternus Takes Over](https://www.gsmarena.com/tim_cook_steps_down_as_ceo_of_apple_john_ternus_takes_over-news-74424.php)** — Cook exits after 15 years running Apple; Ternus, previously SVP of Hardware Engineering, becomes CEO. The same day, [Phil Schiller stepped down](https://www.theverge.com/tech/986869/apple-phil-schiller-stepping-down) from running the App Store and Apple events. Two of Apple's longest-serving executives leaving together is a bigger signal than either move alone. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Qualcomm Raising Chip Prices Starting Tomorrow](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/31/qualcomm-chip-price-increase/)** — Double-digit price hikes on Qualcomm chips take effect for shipments after today; Apple still sources modem chips from Qualcomm for iPhone 17 even as it shifts to its own modems. A cost input worth watching if iPhone pricing moves later this year.

- **[AirPods With Cameras Could Bring Improved Spatial Audio Head Tracking](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/31/camera-airpods-spatial-audio-head-tracking/)** — Code in macOS Tahoe 26.7 points to camera-equipped AirPods that locate the screen you're looking at for better spatial audio. Early, but a sign the next AirPods revision goes beyond a chip bump.

- **[Apple Caught Off Guard by AI Demand for Mac Mini and Mac Studio](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/30/apple-unexpected-mac-mini-and-studio-demand/)** — Local AI/LLM workloads are driving unexpected demand for Apple's small-form desktops. If you're eyeing a Studio or Mini for local model work, expect tighter stock ahead of lower prices. [WAIT]

- **[A Second Tesla Driver Died Stopped on a Freeway With Autopilot/Self-Driving On](https://electrek.co/2026/08/31/tesla-driver-assist-stopped-freeway-mesa/)** — A second case, after one in Florida, of a Tesla sitting stopped in a live travel lane with driver-assist engaged, rear-ended and fatal — and Tesla's own NHTSA data reportedly wasn't disclosed to investigators either time. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Honda and Nissan Team Up to Make the Brains of Future Cars](https://insideevs.com/news/806639/honda-nissan-ecu-partnership/)** — The two automakers will co-develop common ECU hardware and software for vehicles from 2029 onward, a rare cross-automaker software alliance aimed at cutting costs.

- **[Here's Our Best Look Yet at the Volkswagen ID.4 Successor](https://insideevs.com/news/806623/vw-id4-makeover-id-tiguan/)** — Spy shots show a boxier redesign, normal door handles, and updated underpinnings for VW's next electric SUV.

- **[How I'm Using Codex and ChatGPT on My Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/31/codex-report-four-months-of-building-joy-on-my-mac-with-chatgpt/)** — A four-months-in field report on using OpenAI's Codex agent inside the ChatGPT desktop app for real build work on macOS. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
