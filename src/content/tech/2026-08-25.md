Apple's September 9 event is now teed up — a new Mac mini could land within days, ahead of AirPods 5 and the iPhone 18 Pro lineup. OpenAI tightened Codex/ChatGPT Work limits back down for Plus users after weeks of looser caps. Otherwise a quiet news day: one EV range test, one grid-payment expansion, and a heads-up that monitor makers are eyeing the ad-supported playbook TVs already ran.

## What's New

- **[New Mac Mini Reportedly Set to Launch Before iPhone 18 Pro Event](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/24/new-mac-mini-in-coming-days-report/)** — Bloomberg's Gurman says Apple could unveil a new Mac mini "in the coming days," ahead of the Sept 9 iPhone event and before Tim Cook steps down as CEO on Sept 1. Apple has tested both M5 and M6 versions; generation isn't confirmed yet. If you're in the market for a Mac mini, wait a few days. [WAIT]

- **[Apple Reportedly Will Announce iPhone 18 Pro Event Date This Week](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/24/apple-invites-iphone-18-pro-event-soon/)** — Invites are expected this week for a Sept 9 event covering iPhone 18 Pro/Pro Max, a foldable "iPhone Ultra," two AirPods 5 models, and an Apple Watch Series 12. Same event, per [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/24/siri-ai-ios-27-waitlist/), confirms iOS 27/macOS 27 will gate the new Siri AI behind a waitlist at launch — don't expect day-one access when you update.

- **[OpenAI restores 5-hour Codex and Work limits for ChatGPT Plus users](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/24/openai-restores-5-hour-codex-and-work-limits-for-chatgpt-plus-users/)** — After several weeks of a looser weekly-only cap, OpenAI is bringing back the 5-hour throttling window for Codex and ChatGPT Work on Plus plans. If your teams have been running Codex harder than usual, expect that headroom to shrink again. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Ads and tracking infiltrated TVs. Now they're coming for monitors.](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/08/ads-and-tracking-infiltrated-tvs-now-theyre-coming-for-monitors/)** — Monitor vendors are starting to experiment with the ad-supported, tracking-enabled firmware that ruined smart TVs. No specific product yet, but worth remembering next time you're shopping for a display. [READ DEEPER]

- **[The Mercedes CLA 350 4Matic Destroys EPA-Rated Range With Shocking Efficiency](https://insideevs.com/news/805935/mercedes-cla-350-range-test/)** — Real-world 70 mph range testing had the new Mercedes EV going close to 400 miles, well past its EPA rating. A strong efficiency data point for the mainstream EV segment.

- **[More EV Owners Can Now Get Paid To Power California's Grid](https://insideevs.com/news/805919/california-pacific-gas-electric-vehicle-to-grid-charging/)** — PG&E is expanding vehicle-to-grid compensation to more EVs, including the Chevy Bolt EV and Kia EV9. Bidirectional charging is edging toward mainstream utility programs, not just pilot projects. [READ DEEPER]
