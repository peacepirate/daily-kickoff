Only relevant if you're running Tesla + Powerwall, but it's a real capability add, not a software gimmick.

- **[Apple TV's F1 coverage is getting Dolby Atmos](https://www.engadget.com/2279712/apple-tv-dolby-atmos-support-formula-1/)** — Starts with the US Grand Prix weekend, October 23. Minor, but free if you already watch F1 on Apple TV.

## Smart Home Watch

- **[Apple and LG team up on smart home gear: a lock, doorbell, and thermostat](https://www.theverge.com/news/1006238/apple-lg-homekit-rumor-fcc)** — Bloomberg reports Apple is working with LG on a full smart home lineup (video doorbell, thermostat, indoor/outdoor cameras, floodlight camera, smart lock, temperature sensor), LG-branded but Apple-developed, confirmed via FCC and UL filings. It's expected to integrate with Apple's long-rumored smart home hub, which may get announced at an event on October 13. If you've been holding off on replacing a lock or doorbell in your Home Assistant setup, hold a little longer — a week tells you whether this is Matter-compatible or another walled garden.
