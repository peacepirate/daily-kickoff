## TL;DR
Apple's refresh cadence is in motion: the M6 iMac and entry MacBook Pro are already out, and a bigger touchscreen MacBook Pro redesign is being revealed today — worth waiting on if you're Mac-shopping. Bigger story for you directly: ten days after disclosure, Git on macOS still trusts the compromised eMudhra intermediate CAs, and a separate UEFI firmware flaw hits most servers on the planet. On the EV side, CATL's solid-state battery progress and a charger that reuses old EV batteries are the only durable signals.

## What's New

- **[Apple refreshes the iMac, entry-level MacBook Pro with the M6 chip](https://arstechnica.com/apple/2026/10/apple-refreshes-the-imac-entry-level-macbook-pro-with-the-m6-chip/)** — The M6 refresh also brings Apple's new N1 chip for Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6. Already shipped, but see the next item before buying.

- **[Apple's Pro and Tell event: Live updates as we wait to see a MacBook Pro with a touch screen](https://www.engadget.com/2299176/apple-pro-and-tell-macbook-event-live-blog/)** — Apple is revealing a touchscreen, OLED MacBook Pro redesign today in Los Angeles, confirmed by CEO John Ternus's teaser and corroborated across multiple outlets. [WAIT]

- **[Ten days later, Git on macOS still trusts the compromised eMudhra certificates](https://emudhra.we-have-a-problem.com/apple.html)** — Apple disclosed the compromised eMudhra intermediate CAs (details in [Apple's own support article](https://support.apple.com/en-us/149555)), but Git on macOS hasn't dropped trust in them yet. If your teams do code signing or cert-pinned work on Macs, this is still open. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Code-execution flaw in UEFI firmware affects most servers on planet Earth](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/code-execution-flaw-in-uefi-firmware-threatens-most-servers-on-planet-earth/)** — A critical flaw in EDK2 allows attackers to execute code early in the boot process, before the OS or most security controls load. Worth flagging for patch prioritization given the regulated environment you operate in. [SHARE w/ leadership]

- **[iOS 27.2 Adds Selfie-Based Age Verification](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/28/ios-27-2-adds-selfie-based-age-verification/)** — iOS 27.2 also upgraded Apple Photos in two ways ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/28/ios-27-2-just-upgraded-apple-photos-in-two-ways-heres-whats-new/)). Available now via Settings > General > Software Update.

- **[CATL pushes ahead with 20 Ah solid-state EV batteries, with 500 Wh/kg in sight](https://electrek.co/2026/10/28/catl-pushes-ahead-20-ah-solid-state-ev-batteries-500-wh-kg/)** — Small-scale production targeted for 2027. A real range/density signal if you're tracking the EV transition rather than today's lineup.

- **[This 400 kW EV Charger Uses Old EV Batteries To Avoid Costly Grid Upgrades](https://insideevs.com/news/813566/allye-ev-fast-charger-used-batteries-backup/)** — Runs off a simple 16A grid connection by buffering through retired EV battery packs, cutting deployment from months to hours. Relevant to how charging infrastructure scales without grid buildout.
