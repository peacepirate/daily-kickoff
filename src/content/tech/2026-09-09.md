## TL;DR
Apple's holding its first hardware event under new CEO John Ternus today — headline is the $2,000 foldable iPhone Duo and a smaller-than-feared $100 price hike on iPhone 18 Pro. Apple Watch Series 12/Ultra 4 add a privacy-preserving AI day-recap feature worth a look. Elsewhere: a Toyota EV recall for a software power-loss bug, plus two items worth flagging to your team — a record Patch Tuesday and Claude subscriber account theft.

## What's New
- **[Apple's foldable 'iPhone Duo' will reportedly start at $2,000](https://www.theverge.com/tech/991884/apple-iphone-duo-rumor-foldable)** — Apple unveils its first foldable phone at today's event, priced from a reported $2,000. It's also the first launch event run by new CEO John Ternus. [READ DEEPER]

- **[iPhone 18 Pro Price Hike to Be Lower Than Expected](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/09/iphone-18-pro-price-hike-to-be-lower-than-expected/)** — iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max are rising just $100 (to $1,199) rather than the steeper jump many expected, after Apple already hiked Mac/iPad/home-product prices earlier this year over the memory shortage. [WAIT]

- **[The new Apple Watch will use microphone and other sensors to generate AI recaps of your day](https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/09/the-new-apple-watch-will-use-microphone-and-other-sensors-to-generate-ai-recaps-of-your-day/)** — Series 12 and Ultra 4 add an ambient AI feature summarizing your day from mic, GPS, and sensor data, without storing audio — a more conservative design than most always-listening AI wearables. [READ DEEPER]

- **[Toyota Recalls 10,000 C-HR EVs That May Lose Power While Driving](https://insideevs.com/news/807615/toyota-c-hr-recall-lose-power-battery/)** — A software issue in Toyota's newest EV can cause a loss of power; the fix needs a dealership visit rather than an OTA update.

- **[Why this month's Microsoft patch release is a doozy](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/microsoft-patches-a-record-972-vulnerabilities-112-of-them-critical/)** — A record 972 vulnerabilities patched this cycle, 112 critical, with researchers citing an expected rise in AI-assisted attacks. [SHARE w/ engineering team]

- **[Hackers are stealing Claude tokens from subscribers](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/08/hackers-are-stealing-claude-tokens-from-subscribers/)** — Anthropic has warned users after accounts were found consuming tokens with no one actively using them. Worth a heads-up if your teams run Claude subscriptions. [SHARE w/ engineering team]
