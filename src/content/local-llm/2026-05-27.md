# Local LLM Weekly — Week of May 21–27, 2026

*Coverage window: Mon May 21 – Sat May 27, 2026*

## TL;DR

The headline this week is Ollama v0.30.0 entering late pre-release — it drops GGML entirely and rebuilds directly on llama.cpp, with MLX acceleration on Apple Silicon. llama.cpp itself shipped daily builds with Hexagon Q4_1 matmul support and Vulkan matrix decode improvements. Everything else (Open WebUI, LM Studio, r/LocalLLaMA) was silent or blocked this week.

## Releases & Updates

- **[Ollama v0.30.0-rc28](https://github.com/ollama/ollama/releases/tag/v0.30.0-rc28)** — Architecture overhaul: drops the GGML abstraction layer, now integrates llama.cpp directly for inference. GGUF files remain fully supported. MLX used for Apple Silicon acceleration. Two known gaps in this RC: `laguna-xs.2` and `llama3.2-vision` not yet supported. This is the most significant Ollama internals change in a long time — MLX path should directly benefit your 96GB Mac throughput. Watch for a stable v0.30.0 drop soon. **[UPGRADE]**

- **[llama.cpp b9371](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/b9371)** — Removes legacy WebGPU constants (`ggml-webgpu: remove legacy constants`). macOS Apple Silicon arm64 binary available. Routine cleanup build; grab it to stay current. **[UPGRADE]**

- **[llama.cpp b9369](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/b9369)** — WebGPU dispatch fix (`ggml-webgpu: Fix how to dispatch WG to some ops`). macOS arm64 binary available. Incremental correctness fix. **[UPGRADE]**
